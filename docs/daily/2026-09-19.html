    Electrode Geometry-Controlled Quantum Transport in Au–Pentane–Au Molecular Junctions: A DFT–NEGF and Machine Learning Study#
    🧭 物理 / 凝聚态P3 · AI×物理📖 Preprints👤 Vijay Kumar Lamba, Sankit Kassa, Deepika Deepika🔥 AI 6
    相关度

    📖 英文原文

    Molecular electronics offers a route toward continued device miniaturization, but reliable control of charge transport at the molecular scale requires a detailed understanding of the metal–molecule interface. In particular, the role of electrode geometry in determining transport behaviour remains less systematically explored than molecular structure and contact chemistry. Here, we investigate the influence of atomic-scale electrode geometry on the structural, electronic, quantum-transport, thermoelectric, and noise properties of Au–pentane–Au molecular junctions using a combined density functional theory–non-equilibrium Green's function (DFT–NEGF) and machine learning framework. Six representative electrode configurations—Flat Au(111), Pyramidal, Single Apex, Multi-Atom Tip, Nanowire, and Rough Surface—were structurally optimized and systematically compared while maintaining the same pentane molecular backbone and Au–S contact chemistry. The calculations reveal pronounced geometry-dependent variations in Au–S bonding, coordination number, contact area, molecular tilt, charge transfer, electronic-state alignment, and molecule–electrode coupling. These structural and electronic changes directly control quantum transport, with the calculated conductance decreasing from 0.92 G₀ for Flat Au(111) to 0.56 G₀ for the Single Apex configuration. Transmission, current–voltage characteristics, charge redistribution, and interface dipole analysis consistently demonstrate that highly coordinated and extended contacts provide stronger electronic coupling and more efficient transmission pathways. Thermoelectric calculations reveal an opposing trend: lower-coordination contacts exhibit enhanced Seebeck coefficients because of stronger energy dependence of transmission near the Fermi level, whereas the Flat Au(111) junction provides the highest calculated power factor and ZT ≈ 0.68. Shot noise, Fano factor, low-frequency noise, and Hooge-parameter analysis further demonstrate increasing transport fluctuations as electrode coordination decreases. To accelerate transport prediction, a dataset of 1500 configurations were constructed from structural and electronic descriptors extracted from the DFT–NEGF calculations. Among the tested models, XGBoost achieved the highest predictive performance, with a testing R2 of 0.988, RMSE of 0.010 G₀, and MAE of 0.007 G₀. Feature-importance analysis identified Au–S bond length, HOMO–LUMO gap, coupling strength, DOS at the Fermi level, and Au 5d PDOS as the dominant predictors. The trained model successfully predicted conductance for previously unseen electrode configurations, demonstrating its potential for rapid screening of molecular junction structures.

    High-throughput Discovery of Magnetic Rare Earth Transition Metal Alloys#
    🧭 物理 / 凝聚态P1 · 磁性·自旋电子学📖 Preprints👤 Shuo Tao, Osman Goni Ridwan, Liqin Ke, Qiang Zhu🔥 AI 6

    📖 英文原文

    We present an accelerated materials discovery framework that combines diffusion-based crystal structure generation with hierarchical screening to identify new rare-earth–transition-metal magnets simultaneously achieving high magnetization and thermodynamic stability. Using this workflow, we systematically explored over 3000 binary (R-T) and ternary (R-T-T ′) compositions spanning R ∈ Y, Sm, T ∈ Fe, Co, Ni, and T ′ ∈ Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Cu, Zn, and filtered approximately 240,000 generated crystal structures through machine-learning interatomic potential prescreening and spin-polarized density functional theory (DFT) validation. We identify 300+ low-energy magnetic candidates within 0.1 eV/atom above the convex hull at the DFT level, including 5 thermodynamically stable phases. The highest saturation magnetization reaches ∼1.8 T in Fe-rich binary and ternary phases (SmFe 12 , YFe 12 , YFe 18 Ti and Sm 2 Fe 16 Mn). Symmetry analysis reveals that the majority of ternary candidates are subgroup derivatives of known binary prototypes through Wyckoff site splitting that accommodates T ′ substitution. Site-resolved magnetic moment analysis further shows that Mn aligns ferromagnetically with the Fe sublattice with minimal magnetization loss, whereas Cr couples antiferromagnetically, providing systematic guidance for dopant selection. These findings demonstrate a generalizable strategy for targeted magnetic materials discovery and suggest that extending generative searches to larger unit cells (>20 atoms) with higher Fe fractions is a promising route toward stable phases with saturation magnetization exceeding 1.8 T.

    📖 英文原文

    Darma Valley is a high-altitude region in the Dharchula tehsil of Pithoragarh district, Uttarakhand, of the Rang or Darmiya Bhotiya community. Although small, the valley is structurally significant in the interface region along the borders of India, China, and Nepal, and it remains relatively understudied. Darma was one of only six passes officially designated for trade and pilgrimage under the Sino-Indian Agreement of 1954 between the Tibet Region of China and India. The centuries-old trans-Himalayan trading economy that sustained the valley became unviable after the Indo-Tibetan border was closed following the war of 1962, and the region was transformed into a heavily militarised border, guarded by the Indo-Tibetan Border Police. Based on the archival treaty material, colonial-era gazetteers, ethnographic scholarship, and government and news sources ranging from 1816 to 2026, this paper places the valley in the context of the larger frontier complex of Dharchula, located adjacent to the contested Trijunctions of Kalapani, Lipulekh and Limpiyadhara, where multiple interpretations of the 1816 Treaty of Sugauli continue to be a burden on India-Nepal relations with a certain studied ambiguity being displayed by China. In a borderland and securitisation approach the valley is explored as a miniature representation of the governance of the frontier in the Himalayas, where demographic retention, cultural continuity, pilgrimage tourism and infrastructure competition are as crucial in the definition of sovereignty as military presence, as shown in India's Vibrant Villages Programme or China's Xiaokang border village model. The analysis provides a geographically informed example of how human security and agency of the borderlands can be established in high-altitude border areas with contested claims.
