    文献研究：Electrode Geometry-Controlled Quantum Transport in Au–Pentane–Au Molecular Junctions: A DFT–NEGF and Machine Learning Study#
    Electrode Geometry-Controlled Quantum Transport in Au–Pentane–Au Molecular Junctions: A DFT–NEGF and Machine Learning Study#
    作者：Vijay Kumar Lamba、Sankit Kassa、Deepika Deepika。Preprints，2026-09-19
    🤖 AI×科学 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：density functional theory。

    📖 英文原文

    Molecular electronics offers a route toward continued device miniaturization, but reliable control of charge transport at the molecular scale requires a detailed understanding of the metal–molecule interface. In particular, the role of electrode geometry in determining transport behaviour remains less systematically explored than molecular structure and contact chemistry. Here, we investigate the influence of atomic-scale electrode geometry on the structural, electronic, quantum-transport, thermoelectric, and noise properties of Au–pentane–Au molecular junctions using a combined density functional theory–non-equilibrium Green's function (DFT–NEGF) and machine learning framework. Six representative electrode configurations—Flat Au(111), Pyramidal, Single Apex, Multi-Atom Tip, Nanowire, and Rough Surface—were structurally optimized and systematically compared while maintaining the same pentane molecular backbone and Au–S contact chemistry. The calculations reveal pronounced geometry-dependent variations in Au–S bonding, coordination number, contact area, molecular tilt, charge transfer, electronic-state alignment, and molecule–electrode coupling. These structural and electronic changes directly control quantum transport, with the calculated conductance decreasing from 0.92 G₀ for Flat Au(111) to 0.56 G₀ for the Single Apex configuration. Transmission, current–voltage characteristics, charge redistribution, and interface dipole analysis consistently demonstrate that highly coordinated and extended contacts provide stronger electronic coupling and more efficient transmission pathways. Thermoelectric calculations reveal an opposing trend: lower-coordination contacts exhibit enhanced Seebeck coefficients because of stronger energy dependence of transmission near the Fermi level, whereas the Flat Au(111) junction provides the highest calculated power factor and ZT ≈ 0.68. Shot noise, Fano factor, low-frequency noise, and Hooge-parameter analysis further demonstrate increasing transport fluctuations as electrode coordination decreases. To accelerate transport prediction, a dataset of 1500 configurations were constructed from structural and electronic descriptors extracted from the DFT–NEGF calculations. Among the tested models, XGBoost achieved the highest predictive performance, with a testing R2 of 0.988, RMSE of 0.010 G₀, and MAE of 0.007 G₀. Feature-importance analysis identified Au–S bond length, HOMO–LUMO gap, coupling strength, DOS at the Fermi level, and Au 5d PDOS as the dominant predictors. The trained model successfully predicted conductance for previously unseen electrode configurations, demonstrating its potential for rapid screening of molecular junction structures.

    Polarization-coupled complete operators in sliding ferroelectric semiconductors for native in-memory attention computing#
    作者：Xing Wu、di wang、Yukang Liu、Jiabei Huang、Zijing Guo、Yinuo Zou 等 14 人。Preprints，2026-09-19
    🤖 AI×科学 · 铁电·极化AI×科学交叉6方向匹配6
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：ferroelectric。

    📖 英文原文

    Attention computing, central to Transformer-based generative AI, calls for native in-memory hardware with linear and nonlinear operators intrinsically coupled. Current compute-in-memory (CIM), however, remains tailored to linear algebra, lacking integrated nonlinear operators. Here, we demonstrate complete-operator hardware that intrinsically couples both operator types in situ via ferroelectric polarization. Our device integrates a vertical ferroelectric tunnel junction for linear multiply-accumulate operations and a lateral ferroelectric semiconductor field-effect transistor for exponential functions in sliding-ferroelectric semiconductor ReS2. Tuning lateral transport length and vertical barrier thickness yields designable exponential operators with tunable sensitivity and operating range. A fabricated (9×3)×9 array realizes the first in-memory hardware execution of full Transformer attention primitives, including intrinsic Softmax, with device-level 600-ps latency and 320-aJ/operation energy. A closed-loop hardware prototype enables real-time text generation, with experimentally calibrated simulations supporting neural-signal-to-language scalability. This work embeds the missing nonlinearity directly into memory, advancing CIM toward complete, native hardware for generative AI.

    文献研究：High-throughput Discovery of Magnetic Rare Earth Transition Metal Alloys#
    High-throughput Discovery of Magnetic Rare Earth Transition Metal Alloys#
    作者：Shuo Tao、Osman Goni Ridwan、Liqin Ke、Qiang Zhu。Preprints，2026-09-19
    🧲 物理/材料 · 磁性·自旋电子学AI×科学交叉2方向匹配8
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：density functional theory、interatomic potential、magnetic materials、symmetry analysis。

    📖 英文原文

    We present an accelerated materials discovery framework that combines diffusion-based crystal structure generation with hierarchical screening to identify new rare-earth–transition-metal magnets simultaneously achieving high magnetization and thermodynamic stability. Using this workflow, we systematically explored over 3000 binary (R-T) and ternary (R-T-T ′) compositions spanning R ∈ Y, Sm, T ∈ Fe, Co, Ni, and T ′ ∈ Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Cu, Zn, and filtered approximately 240,000 generated crystal structures through machine-learning interatomic potential prescreening and spin-polarized density functional theory (DFT) validation. We identify 300+ low-energy magnetic candidates within 0.1 eV/atom above the convex hull at the DFT level, including 5 thermodynamically stable phases. The highest saturation magnetization reaches ∼1.8 T in Fe-rich binary and ternary phases (SmFe 12 , YFe 12 , YFe 18 Ti and Sm 2 Fe 16 Mn). Symmetry analysis reveals that the majority of ternary candidates are subgroup derivatives of known binary prototypes through Wyckoff site splitting that accommodates T ′ substitution. Site-resolved magnetic moment analysis further shows that Mn aligns ferromagnetically with the Fe sublattice with minimal magnetization loss, whereas Cr couples antiferromagnetically, providing systematic guidance for dopant selection. These findings demonstrate a generalizable strategy for targeted magnetic materials discovery and suggest that extending generative searches to larger unit cells (>20 atoms) with higher Fe fractions is a promising route toward stable phases with saturation magnetization exceeding 1.8 T.

    The Darma Valley at the Himalayan Trijunction: Community, Territoriality, and Security Dynamics in the Borderland of India, China and Nepal#
    作者：Rishabh Verma。Preprints，2026-09-19

    📖 英文原文

    Darma Valley is a high-altitude region in the Dharchula tehsil of Pithoragarh district, Uttarakhand, of the Rang or Darmiya Bhotiya community. Although small, the valley is structurally significant in the interface region along the borders of India, China, and Nepal, and it remains relatively understudied. Darma was one of only six passes officially designated for trade and pilgrimage under the Sino-Indian Agreement of 1954 between the Tibet Region of China and India. The centuries-old trans-Himalayan trading economy that sustained the valley became unviable after the Indo-Tibetan border was closed following the war of 1962, and the region was transformed into a heavily militarised border, guarded by the Indo-Tibetan Border Police. Based on the archival treaty material, colonial-era gazetteers, ethnographic scholarship, and government and news sources ranging from 1816 to 2026, this paper places the valley in the context of the larger frontier complex of Dharchula, located adjacent to the contested Trijunctions of Kalapani, Lipulekh and Limpiyadhara, where multiple interpretations of the 1816 Treaty of Sugauli continue to be a burden on India-Nepal relations with a certain studied ambiguity being displayed by China. In a borderland and securitisation approach the valley is explored as a miniature representation of the governance of the frontier in the Himalayas, where demographic retention, cultural continuity, pilgrimage tourism and infrastructure competition are as crucial in the definition of sovereignty as military presence, as shown in India's Vibrant Villages Programme or China's Xiaokang border village model. The analysis provides a geographically informed example of how human security and agency of the borderlands can be established in high-altitude border areas with contested claims.

    作者：Gayatri Surendra Patil、K. R. Patil。Preprints，2026-09-19

    Drying is among the most energy-intensive operations in food processing, necessitating the development of technologies that balance energy efficiency with product quality preservation. Refractance Window Drying (RWD) has emerged as a promising fourth-generation drying technique for heat-sensitive materials. This study presents the design, development, and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) analysis of a solar-assisted RWD system, complemented by experimental validation. A three-dimensional transient CFD model was developed to investigate coupled heat transfer and airflow characteristics within the drying chamber. Simulation results revealed uniform airflow distribution and stable operating temperatures of approximately 60°C across the drying surface, providing favorable conditions for temperature-sensitive agricultural products. Experimental investigations were conducted using a solar-assisted continuous RWD setup at hot water temperatures of 82, 86, 90, 94, and 98°C with belt speeds of 0.8, 1.1, 1.4, 1.7, and 2.0 m/min. The numerical predictions were validated against experimental data, demonstrating a maximum temperature deviation of approximately 5°C. The results confirm that CFD modeling combined with experimental validation is an effective tool for optimizing solar-assisted RWD systems, thereby improving drying uniformity, enhancing energy efficiency, and preserving product quality compared with conventional drying technologies. The developed approach contributes to Sustainable Development Goal 7 through the utilization of renewable solar energy and to Sustainable Development Goal 12 through improved resource-efficient food processing, while its application for agricultural product preservation has potential relevance to SDG 2 by helping reduce post-harvest food losses.

    Development and Evaluation of Target-Specific Machine-Learning Scoring Functions for Monoamine Oxidase B#
    作者：Sherif Adel Arafa Elsabbagh。Preprints，2026-09-19

    📖 英文原文

    Target-specific machine-learning scoring functions (MLSFs) have improved virtual screening for several targets, but their performance depends heavily on benchmark design and modeling choices. Here, we develop and evaluate target-specific MLSFs for human monoamine oxidase B (MAO-B), systematically comparing three modeling decisions: classification versus regression, structure-based (PLEC) versus combined structure- and ligand-based (PLEC + Morgan fingerprint) featurization, and untuned versus hyperparameter-tuned models. Generic scoring function performance was first established on the DUD-E benchmark, then re-evaluated on an independent test set composed exclusively of experimentally confirmed inactive compounds to control for property-matched decoy bias. On DUD-E, RF-Score-VS achieved the highest early enrichment among generic scoring functions (NEF1% = 0.789), but declined to NEF1% = 0.061 on the true-inactive test set — indistinguishable from the native Smina docking score and close to the random baseline (NEF1% = 0.024). Target-specific models trained on 2,328 actives and 121,764 DeepCoy-generated decoys consistently outperformed their classification counterparts: regression exceeded classification for all five algorithms tested. Combining Morgan fingerprints with PLEC features improved performance in five of ten baseline algorithm–task combinations. The best-performing model was tuned XGB regression with combined features (NEF1% = 0.245), exceeding the best generic scoring function on the same test set (CNN-Score, NEF1% = 0.082) by approximately threefold. Hyperparameter optimization did not reliably improve early-recognition performance, and a structural similarity analysis showed that the 25 test actives with above-threshold training-set similarity were retrieved at a substantially higher rate than the remaining 92 (28.0% vs. 5.4% for XGB; 28.0% vs. 2.2% for SVM), indicating that a meaningful share of early-recognition performance reflects structural analogy to known actives rather than generalization to novel chemical space. These findings argue for carefully designed benchmarks, explicit bias controls, and prospective validation in future target-specific MLSF studies.
