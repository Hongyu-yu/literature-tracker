    发热/吸热流体通过多孔板的不稳定 MHD 流动，温度和浓度在滑流状态下变化#
    Unsteady MHD flow of Heat generating / absorbing fluid through porous plate with variable temperature and concentration in a slip flow regime#
    作者：Shaik Mubarak、M. Suryanarayana Reddy、M. C. Raju。Preprints，2026-10-10
    🧲 物理/材料 · 磁性·自旋电子学AI×科学交叉2方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：本研究研究了在粘性、不可压缩、导电流体的滑流情况下，通过浸入多孔材料中的无限垂直多孔板进行的非定常磁流体动力学（MHD）自由对流传热和传质。该板受到一致的横向磁场的作用。扰动方法为无量纲控制方程提供了解析解。对影响速度、温度和浓度分布的物理参数进行了图形分析和深入讨论。这些参数包括热格拉霍夫数、修正格拉霍夫数、磁参数、磁导率参数、辐射参数、热量产生/吸收、普朗特数、施密特数、化学反应参数以及索雷特数。根据研究结果，速度剖面随着浮力和磁导率参数的增加而上升，而随着磁参数和由于洛伦兹力的阻性滑移效应而下降。热辐射和热源显着增强了温度分布和热边界层厚度，而较高的普朗特数由于较低的热扩散率而减小了温度场。浓度分布随着施密特数和化学反应参数的增加而减小，而由于热扩散效应的增强，浓度分布随着索雷特数的增加而增加。还讨论了控制参数对表面摩擦系数、努塞尔数和舍伍德数等工程量的影响。目前的研究为通过多孔介质的辐射MHD传输现象提供了有价值的见解，并且在地热工程、化学反应堆、核反应堆冷却系统、石油工程、能量传输系统和隔热技术方面具有潜在的应用。

    📖 英文原文

    The present study investigates the unsteady magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) free convective heat and mass transfer flow via an infinite vertical porous plate immersed in a porous material under slip flow circumstances of a viscous, incompressible, electrically conducting fluid. The plate is subjected to a consistent transverse magnetic field. The perturbation approach provides an analytical solution to the non-dimensional governing equations. Physical parameters that affect velocity, temperature, and concentration distributions are graphically analyzed and thoroughly discussed. These parameters include thermal Grashof number, modified Grashof number, magnetic parameter, permeability parameter, radiation parameter, heat generation/absorption, Prandtl number, Schmidt number, chemical reaction parameter, as well as Soret number. According to the findings, the velocity profile rises as the buoyancy and permeability parameters increase, while it falls with the magnetic parameter and slip effects due to the resistive Lorentz force. Thermal radiation and heat source significantly enhance the temperature distribution and thermal boundary layer thickness, while higher Prandtl number reduces the temperature field because of lower thermal diffusivity. The concentration profile decreases with increasing Schmidt number and chemical reaction parameter, whereas it increases with increasing Soret number due to enhanced thermal diffusion effects. The effects of the governing parameters on engineering quantities such as skin friction coefficient, Nusselt number, and Sherwood number are also discussed. The present investigation provides valuable insight into radiative MHD transport phenomena through porous media and has potential applications in geothermal engineering, chemical reactors, nuclear reactor cooling systems, petroleum engineering, energy transport systems, and thermal insulation technologies.

    作者：Adela Mullerova、Zbynek Dzuman、Radek Machat、Sylvie Skalickova、Renata Kucerova、Michal Psurny 等 11 人。Preprints，2026-10-10
    🧲 物理/材料 · 其他AI×科学交叉2方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：霉菌毒素吸附剂通常仅通过毒素结合能力来评价。然而，单独的吸附并不能反映胃肠道条件下的结合稳定性或肠上皮的生物保护。本研究采用综合物理化学和生物学方法，比较了基于膨润土和生物炭的商业和实验吸附剂对 T-2 毒素和玉米赤霉烯酮 (ZEN) 的影响。测试了七种材料：一种商业活化膨润土，两种实验膨润土及其 NaCl 活化变体，以及两种实验生物炭。该评估结合了物理化学表征、连续吸附-解吸（模拟胃到肠的转变）以及使用 IPEC-J2 细胞中的连续跨上皮电阻 (ECIS-TEER) 监测的生物功效评估。氯化钠活化根据母体材料改变膨润土表面特性，而不破坏蒙皂石框架。后续测试揭示了仅通过初始吸附隐藏的毒素保留差异。实验生物炭 1 显示出对两种毒素的完全结合效率，而商业活性膨润土则超过 93%。物理化学结合不能可靠地预测生物保护。商业膨润土可保护两种毒素的屏障功能。实验膨润土 2 和生物炭 1 可防止 T-2 毒素，而实验膨润土 1 则能最好地支持对 ZEN 的保护，其活化形式会改变屏障反应动力学。这些发现表明，吸附能力、结合稳定性和上皮屏障功能的保留代表了吸附剂功效的互补维度。将顺序吸附-解吸与连续 ECIS-TEER 监测相结合，为评估霉菌毒素吸附剂提供了一个全面的框架。

    📖 英文原文

    Mycotoxin adsorbents are usually evaluated solely by toxin-binding capacity. However, adsorption alone does not reflect binding stability under gastrointestinal conditions or biological protection of the intestinal epithelium. This study compared commercial and experimental bentonite- and biochar-based adsorbents against T-2 toxin and zearalenone (ZEN) using an integrated physicochemical and biological approach. Seven materials were tested: one commercial activated bentonite, two experimental bentonites and their NaCl-activated variants, and two experimental biochars. The evaluation combined physicochemical characterization, sequential adsorption-desorption (simulating gastric-to-intestinal transitions), and biological efficacy assessment using continuous transepithelial electrical resistance (ECIS-TEER) monitoring in IPEC-J2 cells. NaCl activation altered bentonite surface properties depending on the parent material without disrupting the smectite framework. Sequential testing revealed differences in toxin retention hidden by initial adsorption alone. Experimental biochar 1 showed complete binding efficiency for both toxins, while commercial activated bentonite exceeded 93%. Physicochemical binding did not reliably predict biological protection. Commercial bentonite protected barrier function against both toxins. Experimental bentonite 2 and biochar 1 protected against T-2 toxin, whereas experimental bentonite 1 best supported protection against ZEN, with its activated form altering barrier-response kinetics. These findings demonstrate that adsorption capacity, binding stability, and preservation of epithelial barrier function represent complementary dimensions of adsorbent efficacy. Integrating sequential adsorption–desorption with continuous ECIS-TEER monitoring provides a comprehensive framework for evaluating mycotoxin adsorbents.

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：本研究研究了在羟基锡酸锌 (ZHS) 催化剂上甘油与 CO2 的直接羰基化反应，在相对温和的反应条件下合成碳酸甘油酯 (GLC)。反应在半间歇式反应器中在 120°C 和 5 bar CO2 下进行 6 小时。采用Zn2+–Sn4+与NaOH共沉淀(ZSCP)、ZSCP进一步水热处理或碱辅助表面处理(CPAST)制备ZHS催化剂，并比较其催化性能。在所检查的催化剂中，由 CPAST 制备的 ZHS 提供了最高的 GLC 产率，为 17.1%，而 ZSCP 衍生的 ZHS 为 15.9%。 XPS分析表明ZHS的表面碱度与Sn4+部分还原为Sn2+以及随之形成的表面氧空位有关。在没有 ZHS 的情况下，甘油主要发生低聚，而 ZHS 则促进 CO2 活化并掺入甘油中形成 GLC。值得注意的是，GLC 产率与哈米特指示剂法测定的 ZHS 催化剂的表面碱度表现出很强的线性相关性，表明表面碱度是控制催化活性的关键描述符。

    📖 英文原文

    This study investigates the direct carbonylation of glycerol with CO2 over zinc hydroxystannate (ZHS) catalysts for the synthesis of glycerol carbonate (GLC) under relatively milder reaction conditions. Reactions were conducted in a semi-batch reactor at 120 °C and 5 bar CO2 for 6 h. ZHS catalysts were prepared by Zn2+–Sn4+ coprecipitation with NaOH (ZSCP), ZSCP by further hydrothermal treatment, or by alkaline-assisted surface treatment (CPAST), and their catalytic performances were compared. Among the catalysts examined, ZHS prepared by CPAST afforded the highest GLC yield of 17.1%, compared with 15.9% for ZSCP-derived ZHS. XPS analysis indicated that the surface basicity of ZHS is associated with partial reduction of Sn4+ to Sn2+ and the concomitant formation of surface oxygen vacancies. In the absence of ZHS, glycerol predominantly underwent oligomerization, whereas ZHS promoted the activation and incorporation of CO2 into glycerol to form GLC. Notably, the GLC yield exhibited a strong linear correlation with the surface basicity of the ZHS catalysts determined by the Hammett indicator method, demonstrating that surface basicity is a key descriptor governing catalytic activity.

    The purpose of this study to investigate the regulatory effects of exogenous 18 lanthanum chloride (LaCl3) on wheat seed germination and seedling physiological 19 characteristics, in order to screen for the optimal treatment concentration that is most 20 conducive to its growth and development.Wheat seeds were used as experimental 21 materials, and a blank control group (CK) and six gradient treatment groups (T1 to T6) 22 were set up, with three biological replicates in each group.     作者：I. Petsagkourakis、O. J. Hagel、A. Sawatdee、S. Carniello、E. Schwarz-Funder、A. Gasslander 等 11 人。Preprints，2026-10-10
    🧲 物理/材料 · 其他AI×科学交叉2方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：导电粘合剂 (ECA) 对于印刷和混合电子产品中表面安装器件的组装至关重要；然而，传统的环氧基系统具有毒性且使用寿命有限。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种基于苯并恶嗪的各向异性导电粘合剂（ACA），其源自生物基二酚酸苯并恶嗪（DPA-Bz）与1,12-二氨基十二烷（DIADO）的共聚，并结合了环保的金属填料。与传统的苯并恶嗪相比，开发的系统能够降低固化温度（~ 150°C），从而提高与温度敏感基材的相容性。从电气角度来看，优化的铜基 ACA 具有低接触电阻和明显的各向异性导电性，其电气性能可与商用环氧基粘合剂相媲美。此外，ACA 在加速老化（85°C / 85% RH）和热冲击测试下保持稳定的电气行为，表明在环境压力下具有强大的可靠性。最后，生命周期评估显示，与参考镍基粘合剂（6.27 千克二氧化碳/千克）相比，全球变暖潜力降低（5.48 千克二氧化碳/千克），并且通过无溶剂配方和增加回收铜含量进一步降低了减排潜力。所提出的基于苯并恶嗪的 ACA 实现了与商业系统相当的电气和机械性能，同时改善了环境性能并降低了毒性，凸显了其作为下一代电子组装的可持续替代品的潜力，并为绿色电子产品开辟了道路。

    📖 英文原文

    Electrically conductive adhesives (ECAs) are critical for the assembly of surface-mounted devices in printed and hybrid electronics; however, conventional epoxy-based systems are associated with toxicity and limited working life. In this work, we present a benzoxazine-based anisotropic conductive adhesive (ACA) derived from a bio-based diphenolic acid benzoxazine (DPA-Bz) copolymerized with 1,12-diaminododecane (DIADO), combined with environmentally preferable metallic fillers. The developed system enables reduced curing temperatures (~ 150°C) compared to conventional benzoxazines, improving compatibility with temperature-sensitive substrates. From an electrical standpoint, the optimized Cu-based ACA exhibits low contact resistance and clear anisotropic conductivity with electrical performance comparable to a commercial epoxy-based adhesive. Furthermore, the ACA maintains stable electrical behavior under accelerated aging (85°C / 85% RH) and thermal shock testing, indicating robust reliability under environmental stress. Finally, life cycle assessment reveals a reduced global warming potential (5.48 kg CO₂/kg) compared to a reference Ni-based adhesive (6.27 kg CO₂/kg), with further reduction potential through solvent-free formulations and increased recycled copper content. The presented benzoxazine-based ACA achieves comparable electrical and mechanical performance to commercial systems while delivering improved environmental performance and reduced toxicity, highlighting its potential as a sustainable alternative for next-generation electronic assembly and opening the path towards greener electronics.
