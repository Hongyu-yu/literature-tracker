    AdsNet：用于直接吸附能量预测的图神经网络#
    AdsNet: A Graph Neural Network for Direct Adsorption Energy Prediction#
    作者：Changzhi Ai、Frank Abild-Pedersen、Johannes Voss。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-20
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：快速准确地预测吸附能对于高通量计算催化剂筛选至关重要，现有方法在评估速度（全图机器学习潜力）或几何灵敏度（基于协调的模型，如 alpha 方案）上进行了妥协。在这里，我们提出了 AdsNet，一种等变图神经网络，可以直接预测单次前向传递中的吸附能，该网络在仅限于吸附原子及其在截止半径内的直接表面邻居的局部图上运行。这种局部图公式将计算复杂性与系统总尺寸解耦，同时对连续几何特征（原子间距离和方向向量）进行编码，从而能够对局部键合环境中的细微变化敏感。 AdsNet 采用消息传递架构，在多个交互层上具有标量和向量节点特征。该模型在 AdsTraj（包含 33,920 个密度泛函理论 (DFT) 计算结构的数据集，涵盖 26 种过渡金属和 6 种吸附物（H*、C*、O*、CO*、OH*、HOCO*））上进行训练，实现了 0.032 eV 的吸附能均方根误差 (RMSE) 和 0.047 eV/A 的力 RMSE。通过自动微分获得的解析力可以通过具有自适应线搜索的投影梯度下降算法实现结构松弛，从而消除整个几何优化。此外，AdsNet 独特地将预测的吸附能分解为局部吸附图中各个原子的贡献——这是传统机器学习潜力无法实现的可解释性特征。因此，AdsNet 为各种材料表面的大规模吸附能预测和局部界面弛豫建立了一个准确、高效和可解释的框架。

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：graph neural network、equivariant graph neural network、machine learning potential、density functional theory。

    📖 英文原文

    Fast and accurate prediction of adsorption energies is critical for high-throughput computational catalyst screening, existing methods compromise either on evaluation speed (full-graph machine learning potentials) or geometric sensitivity (coordination-based models like the alpha-scheme). Here, we present AdsNet, an equivariant graph neural network that directly predicts adsorption energies in a single forward pass, which operates on a local graph restricted only to adsorbate atoms and its immediate surface neighbors within a cutoff radius. This local graph formulation decouples computational complexity from total system size while encoding continuous geometric features (interatomic distances and directional vectors) enabling sensitivity to subtle variations in local bonding environments. AdsNet adopts a message-passing architecture with scalar and vector node features over multiple interaction layers. Trained on AdsTraj, a dataset of 33,920 density functional theory (DFT) calculated structures spanning 26 transition metals and 6 adsorbates (H*, C*, O*, CO*, OH*, HOCO*), the model achieves an adsorption energy root-mean-square error (RMSE) of 0.032 eV and a force RMSE of 0.047 eV/A. Analytic forces obtained via automatic differentiation enable structure relaxation through a projected gradient descent algorithm with adaptive line search, eliminating entire geometry optimizations. Additionally, AdsNet uniquely decomposes predicted adsorption energies into contributions from individual atoms within the local adsorbate graph - an interpretability feature unattainable with conventional machine-learning potentials. AdsNet thus establishes an accurate, highly efficient, and interpretable framework for large-scale adsorption energy prediction and local interface relaxation across various material surfaces.

    📖 英文原文

    Foundation machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) promise near-density functional theory (DFT) accuracy across broad areas of chemistry and materials science. However, their performance in describing systems and processes relevant to heterogeneous catalysis remains underexplored. Here, we introduce HetCat26, a collection of benchmark tests designed to assess pre-trained MLIPs across key aspects of catalytic modeling, including surface energetics, metal-metal oxide interactions, adsorption, and catalytic reaction networks. Evaluating eighteen foundation models, we find that performance on existing general materials benchmarks is only weakly predictive of performance on HetCat26; transferability to heterogeneous catalysis cannot be inferred from these general benchmarks. Three models, eSEN-30M-OAM, SevenNet-Omni-i12 and MACE-MH-1-OMAT, nevertheless achieve high accuracy across the properties evaluated. Beyond model benchmarking, HetCat26 highlights the importance of training data consistency: PBE and PBE+U calculations should not be mixed within a training set. Overall, we identify challenges limiting the transferability of current foundation MLIPs to heterogeneous catalysis and, more broadly, to chemical reactions at interfaces, providing guidance for the development of the next generation of foundation models.

    High-Accuracy Neural-Network Quantum States via Randomized Real-Time Dynamics#
    作者：John M. Martyn、Rod Rofougaran、Samuel Stein、Di Luo。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-20
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 量子信息·计算AI×科学交叉8方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：神经网络量子态（NQS）在能量最小化过程中表现出收敛到基态的差异：虽然能量随变分波函数的误差二次收敛，但其他可观测量仅线性收敛，这限制了物理预测的准确性。在这里，我们展示了如何通过引入一种提高 NQS 准确性的新算法来缩小这一差距。我们的方法称为动态平均，通过对实时演变的状态（或可观察的）进行平均来估计期望值。利用量子信息的见解，我们证明了任意基态可观测量中误差的高达二次抑制，从而匹配能量的快速收敛。重要的是，这种提高的精度既不需要进一步的能量最小化，也不需要增加模型的复杂性，而是利用随机化对量子态的影响。我们在不可积自旋模型上演示了动态平均，它加速了收敛并大大减少了可观测值的误差。

    📖 英文原文

    Neural-network quantum states (NQSs) exhibit a disparity in converging to the ground state during energy minimization: while the energy converges quadratically with the error in the variational wave function, other observables converge only linearly, which limits the accuracy of physical predictions. Here we show how to close this gap by introducing a new algorithm that improves the accuracy of NQSs. Our method, termed dynamical averaging, estimates expectation values by averaging over a state (or observable) evolved in real-time. Using insights from quantum information, we prove an up-to-quadratic suppression of error in arbitrary ground state observables, thus matching the fast convergence of the energy. Importantly, this improved accuracy requires neither further energy minimization nor increased model complexity, but rather exploits the effect of randomization on quantum states. We demonstrate dynamical averaging on non-integrable spin models, where it accelerates convergence and substantially reduces errors in observables.

    强朗道能级混合下的非阿贝尔量子霍尔态：使用第一性原理人工智能寻找任意子#
    Non-Abelian quantum Hall states at strong Landau level mixing: finding anyons with first-principles AI#
    作者：Pierre-Antoine Graham、Ahmed Abouelkomsan、Liang Fu。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-20

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：非阿贝尔任意子是构成容错拓扑量子计算基础的奇异粒子，据预测，它们会在偶数分母填充因子的分数量子霍尔态中出现。事实证明，确定实现了哪种拓扑序是一个具有挑战性的问题，需要对真实的朗道能级混合进行准确的非微扰处理，而这迄今为止在理论研究中尚付阙如。本文首次提出了非阿贝尔量子霍尔问题的完整解，该解包括所有电子和所有朗道能级，适用于填充因子ν = 3/2的自旋极化电子。我们开发了一种动量分辨的神经网络变分波函数，该波函数仅在微观哈密顿量上进行优化，从而可以直接获得拓扑简并基态。在没有任何关于预期结果的物理学输入的情况下，它发现了一个简并基态流形，其纠缠揭示了具有量子维数sqrt2的任意子，这是伊辛型非阿贝尔序的标志。通过直接计算与试探波函数的重叠，我们进一步表明，朗道能级混合更倾向于反普法夫态，而非其他候选态。我们的结果表明，在强朗道能级混合的条件下，神经网络波函数在从第一性原理出发寻找和表征非阿贝尔任意子方面具有强大的能力，这与许多近期实验相关。

    📖 英文原文

    Non-Abelian anyons, strange particles forming the basis of fault-tolerant topological quantum computing, are predicted to emerge in fractional quantum Hall states at even-denominator filling factors. Identifying which topological order is realized has proven to be a challenging problem that requires an accurate, non-perturbative treatment of realistic Landau level mixing, which has so far been missing from theoretical studies. Here we present the first complete solution of non-Abelian quantum Hall problem that includes all electrons and all Landau levels, for spin-polarized electrons at filling factor ν = 3/2. We develop a momentum-resolved neural-network variational wave function, optimized solely on the microscopic Hamiltonian, which gives direct access to the topologically degenerate ground states. Without any physics input about the expected outcome, it discovers a degenerate ground-state manifold whose entanglement reveals anyons with quantum dimension sqrt2, the hallmark of Ising-type non-Abelian order. By directly computing the overlap with trial wave functions, we further show that Landau level mixing favors the anti-Pfaffian over other candidate states. Our results demonstrate the power of neural-network wave functions in finding and characterizing non-Abelian anyons from first principles, in regimes of strong Landau level mixing, relevant to many recent experiments.
