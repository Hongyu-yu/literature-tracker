    作者：Yutian Shen、Liguang Dong、Xiangyan Chen、Aaiza Gul、Jian Chen、Weiting Liu。Preprints，2026-11-21
    🧲 物理/材料 · 量子信息·计算AI×科学交叉2方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：量子表征充当量子硬件与校准、编译、验证和缓解量子硬件的软件之间的信息接口。然而，记录可以是任意精确的，并且仍然忽略控制下游决策的坐标。我们制定决策完整性：当 ker C 包含在 ker A 中时，特征图 C 准确地确定局部决策图 A。该标准是免重构的，并识别声明的局部模型内的最小任务导向增强。对于量子位编织重写，其数据兼容光纤上的平均保真度和单一性是恒定的，而首选电路则相反；因此，这些记录携带的有关该光纤上每个先前支持的决策的相互信息恰好为零。有限误差规则证明左、证明右或弃权。在两次公共时间分辨门组断层扫描采集中，模型分别拟合到每次采集的前半部分，而在第一次采集中选择的选择阈值不变地转移到第二次采集中。最终的选择器在 79 个可判定对上达到了 93.7% 的准确率，而对于大多数相同子集的准确率则为 72.2%。在 151 个固定对上，增加表征深度可将准确度从 29.1% 提高到 90.1%。负面的跨日期、跨模型和软件测试限制了可移植性。在对 14 个公共硬件训练的开放进化模型的单独审计中，773 个相关重写目标中有 654 个在较低拟合误差的一半内发生逆转，不包括该建模系列中与条件无关的查找规则。将通用探针从 7 个设置扩展到 15 个设置可达到完整的训练排名，但不会提高保留排名。因此，仅相对于它必须支持的决策而言，特征描述才是完整的。

    📖 英文原文

    Quantum characterization serves as the information interface between quantum hardware and the software that calibrates, compiles, verifies, and mitigates it. Yet a record can be arbitrarily precise and still omit the coordinate that controls a downstream decision. We formulate decision completeness: a characterization map C determines a local decision map A exactly when ker C is contained in ker A. This criterion is reconstruction-free and identifies the minimal task-directed augmentation within the declared local model. For a qubit braid rewrite, average fidelity and unitarity are constant on their data-compatible fiber while the preferred circuit reverses; these records therefore carry exactly zero mutual information about the decision for every prior supported on that fiber. A finite-error rule certifies left, certifies right, or abstains. On two public time-resolved gate-set-tomography acquisitions, the model is fitted separately to the first temporal half of each acquisition, while a selection threshold chosen on the first acquisition is transferred unchanged to the second. The resulting selector reaches 93.7% accuracy on 79 decidable pairs, versus 72.2% for the same-subset majority. On 151 fixed pairs, increasing characterization depth raises accuracy from 29.1% to 90.1%. Negative cross-date, cross-model, and software tests delimit transportability. In a separate audit of 14 public hardware-trained open-evolution models, 654 of 773 correlated rewrite targets reverse within the lower-fit-error half, excluding a condition-independent lookup rule in that modeled family. Expanding a generic probe from 7 to 15 settings reaches full training rank but does not improve held-out ranking. Characterization is thus complete only relative to the decisions it must support.
