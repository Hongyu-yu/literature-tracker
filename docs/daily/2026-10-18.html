    纠正人工智能生成的虚假刑事指控：补救性结案的人权框架#
    Correcting AI-generated false criminal allegations: a human-rights framework for remedial closure#
    作者：Hanane Merzougui、Xianghe Gong、Yaohui Liu。Preprints，2026-10-18
    🧲 物理/材料 · 其他AI×科学交叉0方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：生成式人工智能（AI）可以捏造有关可识别人员的刑事指控，但法律上的困难并不局限于纠正可见的输出。错误声明可能会持续存在于服务端内存、检索系统、应用程序编程接口传输、风险领域和下游机构决策中。现有的关于数据修正、人工智能供应链、算法可竞争性和模型修正的学术研究解决了这个问题的部分问题，但没有为分布式修复何时完成提供统一的法律测试。本文开发了一种补救性关闭的传播感知标准：一种结构化测试，用于确定人工智能系统的相关阶段的纠正措施何时完成。它将国际和区域人权法、欧盟数据保护法以及商业与人权标准的理论分析与有关记忆、检索、模型编辑和遗忘的技术文献结合起来。中国的《个人信息保护法》被用作有限的可移植性比较测试，一份关于人工智能生成的虚假犯罪指控的公开记录的投诉提供了一个案例场景。该框架需要五个功能：易于接受的挑战和合理的决定；在合理可识别的节点处停止；在适用法律允许或要求的情况下，纠正不准确的记录并重新评估受到重大影响的决定；可验证的实施；以及相应的不再发生保障措施。责任仍然是特定于参与者和操作的，如果考虑到严重性和预期的补救价值，进一步的调查客观上不可能或不成比例，则追踪可能会停止。仅当责任行为者能够提供合理的、可证明的保证，证明已验证的虚假指控已在其权限范围内的合理可识别的、实质性相关的处理节点中得到纠正或包含，并解决了适用的下游影响并记录了残余风险时，纠正才被视为法律上完成的。

    📖 英文原文

    Generative artificial intelligence (AI) can fabricate criminal allegations about identifiable people, but the legal difficulty is not confined to correcting the visible output. A false claim may persist in service-side memory, retrieval systems, application programming interface transmissions, risk fields and downstream institutional decisions. Existing scholarship on data rectification, AI supply chains, algorithmic contestability and model correction addresses parts of this problem but does not provide a unified legal test for when distributed remediation is complete. This article develops a propagation-aware standard of remedial closure: a structured test for determining when corrective action has been completed across relevant stages of an AI system. It combines doctrinal analysis of international and regional human-rights law, European Union data-protection law and business-and-human-rights standards with technical literature on memory, retrieval, model editing and unlearning. China’s Personal Information Protection Law is used as a limited comparative portability test, and a publicly documented complaint concerning an AI-generated false criminal allegation provides a case-informed scenario. The framework requires five functions: accessible challenge and reasoned determination; cessation at reasonably identifiable nodes; correction of inaccurate records and reassessment of materially affected decisions where applicable law permits or requires; verifiable implementation; and proportionate non-recurrence safeguards. Responsibility remains actor- and operation-specific, and tracing may cease where further inquiry is objectively impossible or disproportionate considering severity and expected remedial value. Rectification is treated as legally complete only when the responsible actor can provide reasonable, demonstrable assurance that the verified false allegation has been corrected or contained across reasonably identifiable, materially relevant processing nodes within its authority, with applicable downstream effects addressed and residual risks documented.

    📖 英文原文

    Microplastics (MPs) were investigated in two hydrologically connected river–reservoir systems in central Mexico: the Tepeji River–Requena Reservoir and the Tula River–Endhó Reservoir. Water samples were processed by filtration and oxidative digestion, followed by optical microscopy for morphological and size analysis and micro-Raman spectroscopy for polymer identification. Plastic fragments were observed in all water bodies, mainly with sizes below 100 µm, together with abundant microfibers with lengths of approximately 70–1300 µm. Fiber concentrations were (4(:.0±:0.5)) × 10³ and (6.0 ± 0.8) × 10³ fibers L⁻¹ in the Tepeji and Tula rivers, respectively, increasing to (8.0 ± 0.9) × 10³ and (1.4 ± 0.4) × 10⁴ fibers L⁻¹ in the Requena and Endhó reservoirs. Fragment concentrations were on the order of 103–104 particles L⁻¹ for particles ≥ 20 µm, while the 1–20 µm fraction of suspected microplastics was substantially more abundant. Raman analysis identified PET, PP, PE, and PA, with site-dependent distributions and spectral contributions associated with dyes in several fibers. The results suggest that the reservoirs may act as zones of enhanced MP accumulation within both connected systems. In situ physicochemical measurements and nonlinear optical laser-transmission experiments additionally revealed marked differences between sites, while similar optical responses provide complementary evidence of similarities in contaminant composition within the two connected systems, indicating that MP occurrence must be interpreted together with local hydrological and water-quality conditions.
