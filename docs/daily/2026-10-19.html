    作者：Yicheng Chen、Xiaoyun Yu、Hyejeong Song、Xintian Wang、Zihui Li、Zixi Xia 等 8 人。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-10-19

    Accurate atomistic simulations of materials under electric fields require long-range electrostatics and electrical-response modeling at length scales beyond the reach of conventional first-principles methods. Yet these capabilities remain difficult to reconcile with the scalability of machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs). Strictly local models enable efficient spatial parallelization but neglect long-range interactions, whereas existing electrostatic extensions introduce global dependencies that hinder distributed computation. Here, we overcome this limitation by analytically separating the long-range electrostatic forces and Born effective charges (BECs) into a global electrostatics calculation and strictly local derivatives of learned latent charges. This formulation preserves the spatial decomposability of equivariant MLIPs while enabling distributed evaluation of energies, forces, stresses, and BECs across different boundary conditions. Implemented as DPA4C-LR on the textttnvalchemi platform, the framework scales to 5.2 million atoms on four GPUs at approximately twice the computational cost of its short-range backbone. Trained on energies and forces, DPA4C-LR accurately infers BECs and reproduces the field-dependent infrared spectra of water. We further demonstrate its capabilities by recovering the characteristic slim polarization hysteresis of relaxor cePb(Mg₁/₃Nb₂/₃)O3, which is obscured by finite-size effects in smaller simulations; predicting a room-temperature field-driven ionic conductivity of 6.8 ± 0.7~mS/cm in amorphous ceLi₀.₉NbO₀.₉Cl₄.₁; and modeling interfacial thermal transport across Si/diamond interfaces with slab-corrected electrostatics. By combining long-range physics, electrical-response inference, and distributed computation, this framework extends predictive atomistic modeling toward device-relevant length scales.

    机器学习原子间势的线性响应：来自原始细胞的任意波矢量的声子#
    Linear response for machine-learned interatomic potentials: phonons at arbitrary wave vectors from the primitive cell#
    作者：Thalis H. B. da Silva、Hai-Chen Wang、Miguel A. L. Marques。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-10-19
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他凝聚态AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：使用机器学习的原子间势的声子计算几乎完全是在超级单元中的有限位移下进行的，这种方法仅在与超级单元相称的波矢量上才准确，其误差取决于力常数的范围。在消息传递网络中，这个范围随着层数的增加而增长，对于最深的通用模型来说达到 140 埃。在这里，我们证明，本着密度泛函微扰理论的精神，可以在任何波矢量下在原胞中精确地获得消息传递势的动态矩阵，作为力对布洛赫相位调制的位移的线性响应。仅当相邻原子的特征聚集时才进入该阶段，并且训练后的模型无需修改即可使用。另一种导数可通过分析得出群速度和模式 Grüneisen 参数。我们将该方法应用于 NequIP 和 Prophet，并重现了超级单元的 Hessian 矩阵以达到机器精度。以这些结果为参考，我们发现高通量计算中常用的超级单元会导致声子频率达到十分之几太赫兹的误差，并且这些误差随着超级单元的尺寸缓慢减小，特别是对于深度模型。对于低对称性硅酸盐，超晶胞预测了模型所不具备的动力学不稳定性。取决于色散导数的量（例如低温下的群速度和 Grüneisen 参数）比频率本身受到的影响要大得多。当使用两种方法获得相同的力常数时，对于对称晶体，它们的计算成本是相当的。

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：interatomic potential、high-throughput calculations。

    📖 英文原文

    Phonon calculations with machine-learned interatomic potentials are almost exclusively performed with finite displacements in supercells, an approach that is exact only at wave vectors commensurate with the supercell and whose error depends on the range of the force constants. In message-passing networks this range grows with the number of layers, and reaches 140 angstrom for the deepest universal models. Here we show that the dynamical matrix of a message-passing potential can be obtained exactly in the primitive cell, at any wave vector, as the linear response of the forces to a displacement modulated by a Bloch phase, in the spirit of density-functional perturbation theory. The phase enters only where the features of neighboring atoms are gathered, and the trained model is used without modification. One further derivative yields group velocities and mode Grüneisen parameters analytically. We apply the method to NequIP and Prophet, and reproduce the Hessians of supercells to machine precision. Using these results as a reference, we find that the supercells commonly employed in high-throughput calculations lead to errors of several tenths of a THz in the phonon frequencies, and that these errors decrease slowly with the size of the supercell, in particular for deep models. For a low-symmetry silicate, the supercell predicts a dynamical instability that the model does not have. Quantities that depend on derivatives of the dispersion, such as the group velocities and the Grüneisen parameter at low temperature, are considerably more affected than the frequencies themselves. When both approaches are used to obtain the same force constants, their computational cost is comparable for crystals with symmetry.

Predicting inhibitor coverage is essential for understanding and optimizing surface blocking in AS-ALD.     Predicting how crystal structure influences high-temperature stability remains a key challenge in materials modeling and design. Silicon carbide (SiC), one of the most thermally and chemically stable materials known, provides an ideal system for studying this problem because its many polytypes preserve similar local tetrahedral bonding while differing in long-range stacking geometry. To determine the impact of polytype structure on the high-temperature stability of SiC, we combine phase-coexistence machine-learning molecular dynamics with finite-temperature phonon analysis, enabled by a fine-tuned MACE interatomic potential that accurately describes crystalline, high-temperature, and disordered configurations across multiple SiC polytypes.     关联：与你方向的关键词重合：equivariant neural network。

    📖 英文原文

    The electronic self-energy determines the single-particle Green's function and, through it, quasiparticle energies and spectral functions, but computing it from first principles is expensive and it is a difficult machine-learning target: complex-valued, matrix-valued in a local-orbital basis, and dependent on a continuous frequency. We predict the dynamical correlation part of the imaginary-frequency G₀W₀ self-energy matrix of molecules from atomic species and coordinates with an E(3)-equivariant message-passing network, the rotational covariance of the orbital-space matrix being imposed by the architecture itself and the static exchange part taken from the density-functional reference. Frequency enters either as an explicit network input or through frequency-specific output heads, and the predicted matrices are continued to the real axis in a representation in which causality and a non-negative spectral density hold by construction. The frequency-conditioned model reproduces matrix elements to below 1 meV on the imaginary axis for the systems studied here, and that accuracy survives the continuation: for water clusters, including a tetramer absent from the training set, and for chemically diverse molecules from QM9, the reconstructed spectral functions follow the G₀W₀ reference peak by peak and the frontier quasiparticle levels are recovered to a few tens of meV. Learning the self-energy on the imaginary axis is therefore a practical route to real-frequency spectra: once the model is trained, and given the density-functional reference it is built on, a new geometry costs no explicit G₀W₀ calculation.
