    从平均值中恢复：机器学习原子间势的反问题视角#
    Recovering from Averages: An Inverse Problem Perspective on Machine-Learning Interatomic Potentials#
    作者：Tina Torabi、Cheuk Hin Ho、Christoph Ortner、Ruo Ning Qiu。arXiv · physics，2026-11-23
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：机器学习原子间势 (MLIP) 根据量子力学参考数据近似计算原子系统的势能表面，并以接近从头算的精度实现分子模拟，且成本仅为一小部分。由于它们是根据总能量和力拟合的，因此估计的内容并不明显：总能量或多体势。从数学上讲，这可以表述为一个问题：函数是否可以从随机簇上的平均值中恢复。我们研究了简化统计设置中固定相关阶数的线性模型。我们证明，在人口限制下，潜力在自然加权索博列夫空间中是可识别的，并得出有限样本恢复保证。成对、三体和四体模型的数值实验支持了该理论。

    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：interatomic potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) approximate the potential energy surface of atomistic systems from quantum-mechanical reference data and enable molecular simulation at near-ab-initio accuracy at a fraction of the cost. Since they are fitted to total energies and forces, it is not obvious what is estimated: the total energy or the many-body potentials from which it is assembled. Mathematically, this can be formulated as a question whether a function can be recovered from averages of its values over random clusters. We study it for a linear model of fixed correlation order in simplified statistical setting. We show that, in the population limit, the potential is identifiable in a natural weighted Sobolev space, and derive finite-sample recovery guarantees. Numerical experiments for pair, three-body, and four-body models support the theory.

    📖 英文原文

    Machine-learned interatomic potentials approach density-functional accuracy, but explicit-solvent atom counts are dominated by hydrogens and water. Uniform equivariant architectures still give every atom the same latent width and number of expensive block updates. To our knowledge, no prior method reallocates capacity by chemical role while retaining every atom and its first-principles force. We introduce E2Former-V3, which treats capacity as an atom-level resource: a static chemical role, assigned before the backbone runs, sets each atom's latent width and update count. No atom is removed, no coordinate is integrated out, and every force remains the gradient of one conservative energy. Standard reduces only the update count, sending hydrogen through half as many full-width block updates; on OMol25 validation it reaches a force mean absolute error (MAE) of 8.146 meV/Angstrom, against E2Former-V2's published 8.38, at 1.75-1.84x V2's throughput with lower peak memory on one H200. Across 54 20 ps MD legs, a 100 ps NVE check, and a 1 ns NVT run on a 32,437-atom solvated protein, all runs complete; the MD legs show no non-finite values, clashes, or water fusion, and water O-O and Na-O RDF peak positions match experiment within 0.04 Angstrom. E2Former-V3 therefore moves the accuracy-throughput frontier for all-atom, conservative simulation, not a uniform accuracy win: Standard is worse on biomolecules and metals, better on electrolytes and organics; energy MAE is higher than E2Former-V2's; and water densities remain about 7.5% too high.     Machine-learning molecular dynamics reveals dynamic interfacial structure and active-site accessibility in Fe–N–C single-atom catalysts#
    The Journal of Chemical Physics Current Issue，2026-11-23
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：molecular dynamics、density functional theory。

    📖 英文原文

    Fe–N–C single-atom catalysts are promising Pt-free catalysts for the oxygen reduction reaction. However, molecular-level understanding of their aqueous interfaces remains limited because ab initio molecular dynamics is restricted to small systems and picosecond timescales, whereas existing classical force fields lack the accuracy and transferability required to describe Fe–water coordination and interfacial solvent structure. In this study, we developed an ensemble of equivariant neural-network potentials for the Fe–N–C/water system using spin-polarized density functional theory reference data. Using these potentials, we performed molecular dynamics simulations of up to 5 ns for an interface containing 320 water molecules. Our simulations reveal persistent axial coordination of water at the Fe sites and a locally stabilized hydrogen-bond network. Enhanced sampling shows that coordinated water can exchange between opposite sides of the Fe–N 4 plane through a low-energy pathway with a barrier of ∼0.07 eV, whereas blocking backside solvent access in a bilayer model strongly suppresses this exchange and prolongs site hydration. Constrained ab initio simulations further show that O 2 can approach the Fe site from the side opposite to the coordinated water, while no physically reasonable direct-displacement pathway is identified from the water-coordinated side within the present sampling protocol. Additionally, electronic-response calculations averaged over multiple configurations reveal a contact region with low polarizability, which limits the electronic capacitance of the contact layer under ion-free conditions resembling the potential of zero charge. These results clarify how dynamic hydration governs interfacial structure and active-site accessibility at Fe–N–C/water interfaces.

    📖 英文原文

    Grain boundaries (GBs) govern many properties of polycrystalline materials, and inverse design would make the GB structure itself a design variable. Crystal generative models such as MatterGen operate on periodic cells, which contain two GBs. The cell energy defines a single GB only when the two are equivalent, but existing models do not enforce this equivalence. Building on MatterGen, we developed a symmetry-constrained diffusion model in which the two GBs are related by a symmetry operation: atoms near one GB are generated, and those near the other are reconstructed through a symmetry projection applied at every denoising step. We applied it to the Σ 5(210)[001] symmetric tilt GB in Al, conditioning generation on the GB energy from a machine-learning interatomic potential (MLIP). All 350 generated structures kept a geometrically equivalent GB pair after relaxation. Given a target GB energy, the model generated structures at that energy: for the three in-range targets with many nearby training structures, 44% to 70% fell within ± 0.1 J/m² of the target. Tracking weakened at higher energies and failed outside the training range. Of the 91 near-target structures, 90 matched no training structure under the adopted geometric criterion, indicating that the model can propose configurations near the target energy that are distinct from the training structures, a capability that could help identify unknown metastable GB structures observed in experiments. Building GB-pair equivalence into generation thus makes GB properties well-defined design targets, enabling inverse design of GB structures.

    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：first-principles calculations。

    📖 英文原文

    High-throughput searches for half-Heusler thermoelectrics increasingly rely on lattice thermal conductivity (κ_L) from first-principles calculations, or from machine-learning models trained on them. These calculations describe an ideal single crystal, whereas measurements are made on polycrystalline samples, in which grain boundaries and defects scatter heat as well. The resulting gap between calculated and measured κ_L has been noted for individual compounds but not, to our knowledge, quantified across a materials class, so the values used for screening carry an unknown, systematic bias. Here we quantify and correct this bias for cubic half-Heusler compounds with 18 valence electrons. Using a curated literature corpus that keeps calculated and measured values apart, we show that calculations overestimate measured κ_L in most bonding families, and that the excess shrinks with temperature. Scattering by defects, added to the intrinsic scattering between lattice vibrations, would give such a trend, but the data do not single out one cause. A simple transfer function with this temperature dependence removes the median offset, and refitting its magnitude within a bonding family lowers the median error further, though not consistently compound by compound. Combined with a machine-learning model that predicts the calculated value from crystal structure, and tested on chemistries withheld from all fitting, the method predicts measured κ_L in this class about as closely as independent laboratories agree with one another, and provides testable predictions for compounds not yet measured.
