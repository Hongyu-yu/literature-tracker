    Machine Learning the Complete Active-Space Wavefunction Reconstruction#
    作者：Aniruddha Seal、Bhavnesh Jangid、Helen S. Clifford、Andrew L. Ferguson、Laura Gagliardi。arXiv · physics，2026-11-05
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：interatomic potential、electronic structure、active learning。

    📖 英文原文

    Machine-learned interatomic potentials enable multireference-quality reaction dynamics but do not retain the electronic structure underlying the predicted energies and forces. Recovering this information by independently performing complete active space self-consistent field (CASSCF) calculations is both expensive and potentially inconsistent. We introduce the Complete Active-Space Wave Function Reconstruction (CAS-WR) model, which learns the geometry-dependent electronic structure from CASSCF data and reconstructs the CAS wave function without repeated orbital optimization. Combined with uncertainty-based active learning, CAS-WR is demonstrated for the Diels--Alder reaction and ceTiC+-catalyzed C--H activation. The approach enables trajectory-resolved analysis of multiconfigurational electronic structure across reactive ensembles. Preliminary applications to excited-state proton transfer and transition-metal photocatalysis suggest that CAS-WR can provide a general route toward recovering electronic structure along complex ground- and excited-state reactive dynamics.

    作者：Haihui Wang、Xinyan Wang、Jiashu Liang、Yuanheng Wang、Chang Han、Wen Yan 等 8 人。arXiv · physics，2026-11-05
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配6
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：interatomic potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Accurate reproduction of electronic-structure labels by machine-learning interatomic potentials does not necessarily ensure reliable condensed-phase properties. We investigate the effects of basis-set superposition error (BSSE) and an explicit Axilrod-Teller-Muto (ATM) three-body dispersion contribution on molecular-liquid predictions. Starting from MACELES-OFF, we fine-tune a baseline potential at the ωB97M-V/def2-TZVPD level. A separate branch learns counterpoise (CP) energy and force corrections with the baseline frozen, while a D4-parametrized ATM term is added during molecular-dynamics inference. Mean atom-normalized CP and ATM contributions are 0.222 and 0.205 meV/atom, respectively, for the analyzed SPICE2 configurations from OMol25. We compare baseline, CP-only, ATM-only, and CP + ATM variants for 39 organic liquids. Relative to baseline, the mean system-wise density changes are -1.17%, -10.38%, and -11.93% for CP-only, ATM-only, and CP + ATM, respectively; the corresponding enthalpy-of-vaporization changes are -2.79%, -14.74%, and -18.32%. For the 11 liquids with experimental self-diffusion data, the mean self-diffusion changes are +17.66%, +203.02%, and +230.36%. Both corrections thus lower density and enthalpy of vaporization while increasing molecular mobility on average. ATM dominates these changes, while CP provides a smaller contribution; their combination yields the largest average shifts. These results show that corrections with sub-meV/atom mean values on the analyzed configurations can substantially reshape the predicted structural, thermodynamic, and transport properties of molecular liquids.

    Drift-React: One-step Generation of Reaction Pathways via SE(3) Drifting Fields#
    作者：Rémi Schlama、Philippe Schwaller。arXiv · physics，2026-11-05
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×化学·材料AI×科学交叉6方向匹配0
    📖 英文原文

    Mapping reaction pathways and transition states (TS) is fundamental to chemistry but computationally expensive at scale. The minimum energy pathway (MEP) dictates reaction rates and mechanisms, yet recovering it via electronic-structure methods requires thousands of costly force evaluations. Recent generative models accelerate TS identification but rely on iterative inference and only predict isolated saddle-point snapshots, missing the continuous reaction trajectory. We introduce Drift-React, an SE(3)-equivariant generative framework that predicts complete reaction pathways in a single forward pass from only reactant and product geometries. By shifting distribution evolution to training via a Sinkhorn-weighted drifting field, Drift-React eliminates both the iterative force evaluations of NEB-style methods and the sequential ODE/SDE integration of diffusion and flow matching models. Evaluated on the Transition1x and Halo8 datasets, our one-step model generates physically consistent MEPs that accurately capture energetic bottlenecks and enable arbitrary-resolution sampling along the reaction coordinate. For isolated TS prediction, Drift-React matches the sub-Ångström accuracy of state-of-the-art iterative models while delivering orders-of-magnitude acceleration, clearing a major computational bottleneck for large-scale reaction network exploration.

    Bright coherent X-rays in the gtrsim50 keV range are highly desirable for applications in materials science, high-energy-density physics, and nuclear physics, but are difficult to obtain from present X-ray free-electron laser (XFEL) facilities. Plasma-based afterburners offer a compact route to increasing the electron-beam energy, yet high transformer ratio (TR) operation is vulnerable to hosing instability, which can severely degrade the witness beam quality. We propose using a transversely elliptical driver beam to excite an elliptical nonlinear plasma wake with an axially varying focusing gradient, thereby suppressing the witness beam hosing through betatron decoherence.
