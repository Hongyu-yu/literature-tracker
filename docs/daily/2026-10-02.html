    作者：Simon Heilig、Jens Püttschneider、Mohammad Itani、Asja Fischer、Timm Faulwasser。arXiv · cs.LG，2026-10-02

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：通用机器学习原子间势现在达到了非常小的保留能量和力误差，以至于它们不再预测模型在模拟中的行为。在这里，我们表明，可以根据数学或物理必要性精确的玻恩-奥本海默曲面满足的属性，在没有任何参考计算的情况下对势进行分级。我们将这些性质分为四个系列：对称性和不变性、自洽性和可积性、统计力学平衡和规律性，并将它们变成十四个廉价的探针，每个探针都针对一个精确且独立于化学和参考方法的目标进行认证。因此，每个探测器都会从潜在已经暴露的能量、力和应力中返回一个绝对的、与架构可比的分数，并揭示固定测试集无法揭示的故障。该套件应用于 64 个预训练电位，以可比的报告精度解析了两个数量级的认证质量，并标记了一个微调电位，其参考误差得到改善，而其表面退化，直到分子动力学失效。所有 64 个模型的交互式排行榜可在 https://jhaens.github.io/pescert-bench 上找到。

    📖 英文原文

    Universal machine-learning interatomic potentials now reach held-out energy and force errors so small that they no longer predict how a model behaves in simulation. Here we show that a potential can be graded without any reference calculation, against properties the exact Born-Oppenheimer surface satisfies by mathematical or physical necessity. We organise these properties into four families, symmetry and invariance, self-consistency and integrability, statistical-mechanical equilibrium and regularity, and turn them into fourteen inexpensive probes, each certifying against a target that is exact and independent of chemistry and reference method. Every probe therefore returns an absolute, architecture-comparable score from the energies, forces and stresses a potential already exposes, and reveals failures a fixed test set cannot. Applied to 64 pretrained potentials, the suite resolves two orders of magnitude of certified quality at comparable reported accuracy, and flags a fine-tuned potential whose reference errors improve while its surface degrades until molecular dynamics fails. An interactive leaderboard of all 64 models is available at https://jhaens.github.io/pescert-bench.
