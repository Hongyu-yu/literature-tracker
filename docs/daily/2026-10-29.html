    A Drude-Augmented Kramers–Kronig Framework for Predicting Optical Properties of Substitutionally Doped Low-Dimensional Semiconductors: Application to (8,0) SiC Nanotubes#
    作者：Abebe Mulatu。Preprints，2026-10-29
    🧲 物理/材料 · 铁电·极化AI×科学交叉2方向匹配3
    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：density functional theory。

    📖 英文原文

    We present a generalizable computational framework that combines a Kramers--Kronig transformation with a Drude free-carrier term to predict the optical and dielectric properties of substitutionally doped low-dimensional semiconductors. The framework is designed as a fast-screening alternative to full density functional theory (DFT) calculations, requiring only three physically transparent input parameters: the effective bandgap energy, the peak absorption energy, and the static dielectric constant. For metallic dopants, a Drude term is added with plasma frequency and scattering rate taken from optical fits of post-transition metals. The framework is validated against published first-principles DFT results for Al-, N-, and P-doped SiC nanotubes and against spectroscopic ellipsometry data for bulk 3C-SiC and 4H-SiC, showing good quantitative agreement. As a demonstration, we apply the framework to 15 dopant configurations of the pristine zigzag (8,0) silicon carbide nanotube (SiCNT), covering Group~III (B, Al, Ga, In, Tl), Group~V (N, P, As, Sb, Bi), and Group~VII (F, Cl, Br, I) elements. The framework captures the p-type to plasmonic transition in the Group~III series (absorption onset shifts from 2.0~eV to 1.0~eV), the monotonic bandgap narrowing in the Group~V series (sim3.7~eV for N to sim1.2~eV for Bi), and the systematic redshift in the halogen series (1.85~eV for pristine to 1.43 ± 0.20~eV for Cl). The framework provides a reproducible, computationally inexpensive route to optical property prediction for doped low-dimensional systems and yields quantitative guidelines for UV photodetectors, visible-range photocatalysts, infrared sensors, and broadband plasmonic devices. Complete Python source code, material parameter database, and figure generation scripts are provided for full reproducibility.
