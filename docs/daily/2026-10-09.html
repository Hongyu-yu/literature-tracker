    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：机器学习原子间势（MLIP）通过访问前所未有的长度和时间尺度正在改变原子模拟。虽然预训练的等变图神经网络在广泛的化学空间中实现了近平衡特性的鲁棒零样本性能，但它们向复杂材料动力学的转化仍然受到分布外反应状态、表示偏差和计算缩放限制的根本挑战。在这篇综述中，我们研究了物理驱动的专业化如何将 MLIP 的适用性扩展到复杂的动力系统。我们系统地评估了 MLIP 设计中的结构权衡：高度紧张配置的欠采样、高阶消息传递架构的令人望而却步的计算开销，以及开放和场耦合系统非局部交互的必要性。通过四个要求严格的应用环境——电气化接口、成分波动的开放系统、多相演化和大规模断裂——我们建立了一个可观测特定验证的框架。我们强调通用基础模型和特定任务潜力的互补作用，强调有针对性的适应必须根据预期的可观察结果进行严格的基准测试。最后，我们提出了开发物理一致、硬件感知力场的路线图，将电子结构精度与宏观材料现象无缝连接。

    📖 英文原文

    Machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) are transforming atomistic simulations by accessing unprecedented length and time scales. While pretrained equivariant graph neural networks achieve robust zero-shot performance for near-equilibrium properties across broad chemical spaces, their translation to complex materials dynamics remains fundamentally challenged by out-of-distribution reactive states, representation biases, and computational scaling limits. In this Review, we examine how physics-driven specialization extends the applicability of MLIPs to complex dynamical systems. We systematically evaluate the structural trade-offs in MLIP design: the undersampling of highly strained configurations, the prohibitive computational overhead of high-order message-passing architectures, and the necessity of nonlocal interactions for open and field-coupled systems. Through four demanding application contexts - electrified interfaces, compositionally fluctuating open systems, multiphase evolution, and large-scale fracture - we establish a framework for observable-specific validation. Highlighting the complementary roles of universal foundation models and task-specific potentials, we emphasize that targeted adaptations must be rigorously benchmarked against intended observables. We conclude with a roadmap for developing physically consistent, hardware-aware force fields that seamlessly connect electronic-structure accuracy to macroscopic materials phenomena.

    Assessing the Reliability of Foundational Machine Learning Potentials for Evaluating the Veracity of the Crystallography Open Database#
    作者：Justinas Šlepavičius、Antanas Vaitkus、Andrius Merkys、Saulius Gražulis、Linas Vilčiauskas。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-10-09

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：晶体学开放数据库 (COD) 包含超过 50 万个通过实验确定的晶体结构。晶体学数据的验证在三个层次上进行，其中第三个层次评估结构的物理合理性。这一水平通常依赖于经验启发法或专家判断，考虑到晶体学数据库的规模和新结构沉积的速度，这些判断速度缓慢且容易出错。在这里，我们评估通用机器学习原子间势（MLIP）是否可以自动执行此级别的验证。在 522 086 个 COD 条目中，317 457 个（60.8%）通过了数据质量过滤器，去除了无序结构、配方不匹配和未建模溶剂。这些结构通过 M3GNet 得到松弛，并且 314 153 (99.0%) 收敛，而细胞体积没有大的变化。对于大多数条目来说，宽松的交易量和存入的交易量几乎相同。因此，收敛和体积变化为识别不能确认沉积结构的弛豫提供了简单的标准。对 327 个结构的手动检查表明，没有单一描述符阈值（能量、最大力或原子位移）可以区分有效结构和无效结构。 M3GNet、CHGNet、PET-MAD 和 PET-OAM 在 524 个选定结构上的比较揭示了模型特定的失败：M3GNet 扭曲了环戊二烯基和其他 π 配体，M3GNet 和 CHGNet 都扭曲了噻吩和噻唑环，而 PET 模型保留了这些基序。所有模型都检测到缺失的氢原子，至少有一些检测到假氢、错误分配的原子类型以及两个先前未报告的坐标错误。没有一个模型能够检测到所有错误类型。因此，MLIP 松弛是一种有用但依赖于模型的筛选工具，并且组合多个模型优于依赖任何单个模型。

    📖 英文原文

    The Crystallography Open Database (COD) contains over half a million experimentally determined crystal structures. Validation of crystallographic data proceeds at three hierarchical levels, the third of which assesses the physical plausibility of structures. This level has typically relied on empirical heuristics or expert judgment, which are slow and error-prone given the size of crystallographic databases and the rate at which new structures are deposited. Here we assess whether universal machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) can automate this level of validation. Of the 522 086 COD entries, 317 457 (60.8%) passed a data-quality filter removing disordered structures, formula mismatches and unmodelled solvent. These structures were relaxed with M3GNet, and 314 153 (99.0%) converged without a large change in cell volume. For most entries the relaxed and deposited volumes are nearly identical. Convergence and volume change thus provide simple criteria for identifying relaxations that do not confirm the deposited structure. A manual inspection of 327 structures showed that no single descriptor threshold (energy, largest force, or atomic displacement) separates valid from invalid structures. A comparison of M3GNet, CHGNet, PET-MAD and PET-OAM on 524 selected structures revealed model-specific failures: M3GNet distorts cyclopentadienyl and other π ligands, and both M3GNet and CHGNet distort thiophene and thiazole rings, whereas the PET models preserve these motifs. All models detected missing hydrogen atoms, and at least some detected spurious hydrogens, incorrectly assigned atom types, and two previously unreported coordinate errors. No single model detected all error types. MLIP relaxation is therefore a useful but model-dependent screening tool, and combining several models is preferable to relying on any single one.

    📖 英文原文

    Accurate prediction of temperature-dependent elasticity is important for assessing refractory carbides under high-temperature conditions, but the computational cost of ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) limits systematic investigations across compositions and temperatures. Universal machine-learning interatomic potentials (uMLIPs) offer an efficient alternative, yet their accuracy for this task remains insufficiently established. Here, we benchmark nine uMLIPs against consistent AIMD reference data for five binary and two high-entropy (HE) carbides between 300 and 1200 K. Elastic constants and the corresponding bulk, shear, and Young's moduli are obtained using stress-strain molecular dynamics, explicitly sampling thermal atomic motion and anharmonic effects beyond thermal expansion alone. We assess absolute elastic properties and normalized thermal softening separately. MACE-MH-1 achieves the lowest overall mean absolute percentage error (5.7%). MACE-MH-1 and DPA4-Mini reproduce thermal softening most accurately, with mean deviations of 2.5 and 2.3 percentage points, respectively. All models generally underestimate stiffness, with larger equilibrium volumes relative to the AIMD reference likely contributing to this trend for most models. C12 exhibits the largest model-dependent errors. The HE carbides are described with accuracy comparable to that of the binary carbides, indicating no apparent accuracy penalty from chemical complexity. Accuracy instead varies with transition-metal composition, with group-V carbides, particularly TaC, presenting the greatest challenge. These results identify promising pretrained models for finite-temperature elasticity in carbides and show why accurate absolute stiffness and thermal softening must be assessed independently.

    投影 Hessian 学习：快速曲率监督，实现准确的机器学习原子间势#
    Projected Hessian learning: fast curvature supervision for accurate machine-learning interatomic potentials#
    作者：Austin Rodriguez、Justin S Smith、Sakib Matin、Nicholas Lubbers、Kipton Barros、Jose L Mendoza-Cortes。Machine Learning: Science and Technology，2026-10-09
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配6

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：二阶导数的 Hessian 矩阵包含比单独的能量和力更丰富的关于势能表面的局部几何形状的信息。尽管将完整的 Hessian 矩阵纳入机器学习原子间势 (MLIP) 训练中可以显着提高准确性和鲁棒性，但显式构建和存储 Hessian 矩阵的二次计算和内存成本限制了其实际使用。在这里，我们介绍投影 Hessian 学习 (PHL)，这是一种可扩展的二阶训练框架，仅使用 Hessian 向量积 (HVP) 来合并曲率信息。通过避免显式 Hessian 构造，而是沿着随机探测方向投影曲率，PHL 将二阶导数监督的成本降低到接近力级的复杂性。由此产生的密集探针轨迹估计器是无偏的，并且随着系统大小表现出良好的缩放性，从而无需二次内存增长即可实现曲率通知训练。我们在理论水平 B97XD/6-31G(d) 生成的反应物、产物、过渡态、固有反应坐标和法向模式采样几何形状的化学多样性数据集上评估了各种训练方法。考虑四种训练方案：能量-力训练 (E-F)、两种基于 HVP 的方法（具有单热或密集高斯向量的 E-F-HVP）和能量-力-Hessian 训练 (E-F-H)。当每个小批量的探针向量被随机化时，两种基于 HVP 的方法都实现了与完整 Hessian 训练相当的能量、力和 Hessian 精度，同时为这里研究的小分子系统提供了超过每个周期的加速。在每个分子只有一个 HVP 的更现实的固定矢量方案中，密集高斯投影始终优于单柱探测，特别是对于远离平衡的几何形状。尽管 Rademacher 探针具有较低的单探针估计方差和有界分量幅度，但使用 Rademacher 探针进行配对后续训练相对于原始高斯选择，模型精度没有出现统计学上的显着变化。总体而言，PHL 有效地用力复杂性曲率训练取代了显式 Hessian 监督，保留了完整二阶方法的大部分准确性优势，同时为更大、更复杂的分子系统提供了可扩展的 MLIP 开发。

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：interatomic potential。

    📖 英文原文

    The Hessian matrix of second derivatives contains substantially richer information about the local geometry of the potential energy surface than energies and forces alone. Although incorporating full Hessians into machine-learning interatomic potential (MLIP) training can significantly improve accuracy and robustness, the quadratic computational and memory cost of explicitly constructing and storing Hessian matrices has limited its practical use. Here, we introduce Projected Hessian Learning (PHL), a scalable second-order training framework that incorporates curvature information using only Hessian–vector products (HVPs). By avoiding explicit Hessian construction and instead projecting curvature along stochastic probe directions, PHL reduces the cost of second-derivative supervision to near force-level complexity. The resulting dense-probe trace estimator is unbiased and exhibits favorable scaling with system size, enabling curvature-informed training without quadratic memory growth. We evaluated various training approaches on a chemically diverse dataset of reactants, products, transition states, intrinsic reaction coordinates, and normal-mode sampled geometries generated at the theoretical level B97XD/6-31G(d). Four training schemes are considered: energy-force training (E-F), two HVP-based approaches (E-F-HVP with one-hot or dense Gaussian vectors) and energy-force-Hessian training (E-F-H). When probe vectors are randomized for each minibatch, both HVP-based approaches achieve energy, force, and Hessian accuracy comparable to full Hessian training while providing more than speedups per epoch for the small molecular systems studied here.
