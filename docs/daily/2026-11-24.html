    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：电池、燃料电池、电解槽和电催化反应器通常单独讨论，但它们往往在同一个地方失效：界面。本综述借鉴了 2019 年至 2026 年间发表的 102 篇开放获取文章，追踪了细胞和催化剂的线索，并收集了其中报告的定量结果。无机固体电解质的电导率范围为 10⁻⁵ 至 10⁻² S cm⁻1（具体取决于类别），达到最高值的硫化物很容易出现界面和水分问题。液碱、阴离子交换和质子交换电池中电解槽的运行温度为 40–90°C，固体氧化物电池中电解槽的运行温度为 600–850°C。对于碱性介质中的氧气析出，镍铁羟基氧化物在 100 和 500 mA cm-2 下需要 251 和 291 mV 的过电位，并运行超过 300 小时，而钴-二茂铁框架在 10 mA cm-2 下需要约 190 mV；两者都是通过在操作过程中重构预催化剂而形成的。单原子催化剂接近 100% 原子利用率，但此处引用的最长实验室稳定性测试超过 300 小时，低于 PEM 电解槽目标 80,000 小时寿命的 0.4%。在 CO2 电解槽中，电流密度可达到 200 mA cm⁻² 以上，但盐沉淀限制了运行。我们将六种催化剂家族与操作表征和机器学习进行比较，并得出结论，标准化报告、设备相关测试和工作状态活性分配现在比新组合物更重要。

    📖 英文原文

    Batteries, fuel cells, electrolyzers and electrocatalytic reactors are usually discussed separately, yet they tend to fail at the same kind of place: an interface. This review draws on 102 open-access articles published between 2019 and 2026, follows that thread across cells and catalysts, and collects the quantitative findings reported in them. Inorganic solid electrolytes span conductivities of 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻² S cm⁻¹ depending on class, and the sulfides that reach the highest values are prone to interface and moisture problems. Electrolyzers run at 40–90°C in liquid-alkaline, anion-exchange and proton-exchange cells and at 600–850°C in solid-oxide cells. For oxygen evolution in alkaline media, a nickel–iron oxyhydroxide needs overpotentials of 251 and 291 mV at 100 and 500 mA cm⁻² and runs for more than 300 h, and a cobalt–ferrocene framework needs about 190 mV at 10 mA cm⁻²; both are formed by reconstruction of a precatalyst during operation. Single-atom catalysts approach 100% atom utilization, yet the longest laboratory stability test quoted here, over 300 h, is under 0.4% of the 80,000 h lifetime targeted for PEM electrolyzers. In CO₂ electrolyzers, current densities above 200 mA cm⁻² are reached, but salt precipitation limits operation. We compare six catalyst families together with operando characterization and machine learning, and conclude that standardized reporting, device-relevant testing and assignment of activity to the working state now matter more than new compositions.

    📖 英文原文

    The Feedback Vertex Set} (FVS) problem asks for a minimum-cardinality set of vertices whose removal makes a graph acyclic. A natural integer-programming formulation enforces one constraint per cycle, but enumerating all simple cycles quickly becomes a bottleneck when constructing QUBO and Ising Hamiltonians. We study undirected graphs and present a hybrid constraint-generation pipeline that starts from a compact minimum-cycle-basis encoding, uses the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) as a heuristic candidate generator, and augments the constraint set only when the residual graph exposes a violated cycle. A lightweight classical verification and pruning stage ensures that the final reported solution is a valid FVS. The study centers on the tradeoff between compact encodings and explicit repair: minimum-cycle-basis constraints reduce binary-variable overhead, but they do not in general enforce all cycle-hitting inequalities, so an iterative augmentation loop remains necessary. We therefore frame the method as a hybrid constrained-optimization workflow rather than a claim of quantum advantage, and evaluate it through simulator-based case studies, exact classical references on small instances, and resource metrics that track constraint growth and slack-variable overhead.
