    从代数完备性到轨道分离：全局神经网络势能面的递归分离不变量#
    From Algebraic Completeness to Orbit Separation: Recursive Separating Invariants for Global Neural Network Potential Energy Surfaces#
    作者：Yiping Hao、Jing Lou、Bina Fu、Dong H. Zhang。arXiv · physics，2026-10-16
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：置换不变多项式 (PIP) 为全局势能面 (PES) 提供了系统可改进的、保持对称性的表示，但其作为神经网络描述符的高计算成本限制了其在大型分子系统中的应用。基本不变量 (FI) 形成代数完备的生成集，大大减少了描述符的数量，并使神经网络 PES 能够用于更大的系统。然而，随着分子尺寸和复杂性的增加，PES 评估的计算成本仍然很高。在这里，我们引入了递归分离不变量（RSI），将描述符设计原则从代数完备性转变为轨道分离。 RSI 是通过相关顺序和递归深度系统构建的，提供了计算效率更高的表示。反应性和非反应性系统的基准测试表明，基于 RSI 的神经网络 (RSI-NN) PES 的评估速度比 FI-NN 同类产品快大约一个数量级，并且系统相关的预测精度在多种情况下相当。这些结果确立了轨道分离作为为全局神经网络 PES 开发有效的对称性保持描述符的有前途的原理。

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：neural network potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Permutationally invariant polynomials (PIPs) provide a systematically improvable, symmetry-preserving representation for global potential energy surfaces (PESs), but their high computational cost as neural network descriptors limits applications to large molecular systems. Fundamental invariants (FIs), which form an algebraically complete generating set, substantially reduce the number of descriptors and enable neural network PESs for larger systems. However, PES evaluation remains computationally expensive as molecular size and complexity increase. Here, we introduce recursive separating invariants (RSIs), shifting the descriptor design principle from algebraic completeness to orbit separation. RSIs are constructed systematically through correlation order and recursion depth, providing a more computationally efficient representation. Benchmarks on reactive and nonreactive systems demonstrate that RSI-based neural network (RSI-NN) PESs achieve approximately an order-of-magnitude faster evaluation than their FI-NN counterparts, with system-dependent prediction accuracy that is comparable in several cases. These results establish orbit separation as a promising principle for developing efficient symmetry-preserving descriptors for global neural network PESs.

    使用复值变分自编码器从光谱诱导极化数据中学习弛豫时间分布#
    Learning relaxation time distributions from spectral induced polarization data with a complex-valued variational autoencoder#
    作者：Charles L. Bérubé、Sébastien Gagnon、Lahiru M. A. Nagasingha、Jean-Luc Gagnon、E. Rachel Kenko、Reza Ghanati 等 7 人。arXiv · physics，2026-10-16

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：光谱诱导偏振（ SIP ）是一种用于表征地下材料的地球物理方法。它通过施加交流电来测量岩石和土壤的频率相关的复杂电阻率。Debye和Warburg分解是分析和解释SIP数据的标准方法，因为它们允许估计几何材料的弛豫时间分布（ RTD ）。然而，传统的分解方法独立处理测量，尽管SIP数据具有复杂值性质，但在实值空间中工作，并提供有限的不确定性量化。这些限制降低了常规分解在异构数据集上的有效性。我们将分解方法重新表述为无监督机器学习问题，并引入条件变分自编码器（ CVAE ） ，该编码器学习从SIP数据集到连续RTD的共享映射。该模型在包括140个实验室和现场粒状混合物、沉积岩和火成岩以及水泥材料的SIP测量的数据集上进行了验证。CVAE在复值数据空间中运行，电阻率的虚部和相位分量的重建误差分别为0.53%和0.27% ，有证据表明在0.007和0.06的相应p值上比传统方法有所改进。推断出的RTD稳定且物理一致，其总充电性和平均弛豫时间分别与可极化晶粒含量和晶粒尺寸相关，测定系数高达0.98。所提出的方法的另一个贡献是学习的潜在表示，它将SIP谱组织成一个结构化空间。相对于传统RTD参数，该空间的二维投影中的无监督聚类将轮廓分数提高了近两倍。

    📖 英文原文

    Spectral induced polarization (SIP) is a geophysical method used to characterize subsurface materials. It measures the frequency-dependent complex electrical resistivity of rocks and soils through the application of an alternating current. Debye and Warburg decompositions are standard methods for analyzing and interpreting SIP data, as they allow estimation of the relaxation time distribution (RTD) of geomaterials. However, conventional decomposition approaches treat measurements independently, work in real-valued spaces despite the complex-valued nature of SIP data, and provide limited uncertainty quantification. These limitations reduce the effectiveness of conventional decomposition on heterogeneous datasets. We reformulate the decomposition approach as an unsupervised machine learning problem and introduce a conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE) that learns a shared mapping from SIP datasets to continuous RTDs. The model is validated on a dataset comprising 140 laboratory and field SIP measurements of granular mixtures, sedimentary and igneous rock, and cementitious materials. The CVAE operates in complex-valued data space and achieves reconstruction errors of 0.53 % and 0.27 % for the imaginary and phase components of resistivity, respectively, with evidence of improvement over conventional methods at corresponding p-values of 0.007 and 0.06. The inferred RTDs are stable and physically consistent, and their total chargeability and mean relaxation time correlate with polarizable grain content and grain size, respectively, with coefficients of determination up to 0.98. An additional contribution of the proposed method is the learned latent representation, which organizes SIP spectra into a structured space. Unsupervised clustering in a two-dimensional projection of this space improves the silhouette score by nearly a factor of two relative to conventional RTD parameters.
