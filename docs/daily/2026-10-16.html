    从代数完备性到轨道分离：全局神经网络势能面的递归分离不变量#
    From Algebraic Completeness to Orbit Separation: Recursive Separating Invariants for Global Neural Network Potential Energy Surfaces#
    作者：Yiping Hao、Jing Lou、Bina Fu、Dong H. Zhang。arXiv · physics，2026-10-16
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：置换不变多项式 (PIP) 为全局势能面 (PES) 提供了系统可改进的、保持对称性的表示，但其作为神经网络描述符的高计算成本限制了其在大型分子系统中的应用。基本不变量 (FI) 形成代数完备的生成集，大大减少了描述符的数量，并使神经网络 PES 能够用于更大的系统。然而，随着分子尺寸和复杂性的增加，PES 评估的计算成本仍然很高。在这里，我们引入了递归分离不变量（RSI），将描述符设计原则从代数完备性转变为轨道分离。 RSI 是通过相关顺序和递归深度系统构建的，提供了计算效率更高的表示。反应性和非反应性系统的基准测试表明，基于 RSI 的神经网络 (RSI-NN) PES 的评估速度比 FI-NN 同类产品快大约一个数量级，并且系统相关的预测精度在多种情况下相当。这些结果确立了轨道分离作为为全局神经网络 PES 开发有效的对称性保持描述符的有前途的原理。

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：neural network potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Permutationally invariant polynomials (PIPs) provide a systematically improvable, symmetry-preserving representation for global potential energy surfaces (PESs), but their high computational cost as neural network descriptors limits applications to large molecular systems. Fundamental invariants (FIs), which form an algebraically complete generating set, substantially reduce the number of descriptors and enable neural network PESs for larger systems. However, PES evaluation remains computationally expensive as molecular size and complexity increase. Here, we introduce recursive separating invariants (RSIs), shifting the descriptor design principle from algebraic completeness to orbit separation. RSIs are constructed systematically through correlation order and recursion depth, providing a more computationally efficient representation. Benchmarks on reactive and nonreactive systems demonstrate that RSI-based neural network (RSI-NN) PESs achieve approximately an order-of-magnitude faster evaluation than their FI-NN counterparts, with system-dependent prediction accuracy that is comparable in several cases. These results establish orbit separation as a promising principle for developing efficient symmetry-preserving descriptors for global neural network PESs.

    📖 英文原文

    Understanding fracture behavior is critical for assessing the structural integrity and longevity. Highly time-consuming physics-based fracture simulations motivate the development of machine-learning-based fracture evolution prediction with surrogate models. However, most existing machine-learning models for fracture prediction operate as black boxes and require large training datasets to generalize reliably. In this work, Thermodynamically Consistent Latent Dynamics Identification (tLaSDI) for parametric systems is introduced to predict fracture patterns and evolution in heterogeneous materials. The model learns the thermodynamic state variable in a lower-dimensional latent space using parametric GENERIC Formalism-Informed Neural Networks (pGFINNs) that enforce key thermodynamic principles, such as total energy conservation and entropy generation. This model embeds physics into the neural network architecture using a hard-constraint method that inherently complies with the underlying thermodynamic principles. The proposed tLaSDI-pGFINNs is further enhanced by a Geometry-Conditioned Autoencoder in learning the geometry configuration for effective geometry-guided learning. The energy and entropy evolution histories demonstrate that the model preserves the thermodynamic constraints on unseen testing samples. The results further show that tLaSDI-pGFINNs can more accurately capture both fracture patterns and the subsequent fracture evolution than the purely data-driven Autoencoder Long Short-Term Memory (AE-LSTM) model.

    We study interlayer exciton superfluidity versus layer separation in two-dimensional electron-hole bilayers at zero magnetic field and quantum Hall bilayers at total filling ν_T=1. Unscreened Hartree-Fock theory predicts coherence at every finite separation, with an energy scale decaying inversely with separation at large distances. We examine how screening, correlations, and spatial instabilities modify this prediction. At zero field, we solve the linearized gap equation with the full momentum and frequency dependence of the interaction screened within the random-phase approximation, without an imposed cutoff. The apparent plasmon enhancement of pairing in the ladder approximation is largely cancelled by the second-order crossed-rung correction. Static screening determines the leading pairing exponent in the controlled high-density limit at fixed separation. At large separation, the pairing scale falls exponentially with squared separation while the instability persists. Extending this result to fixed density assumes Fermi-liquid layers and higher-order dynamical corrections subleading to the static pairing interaction. At high field, we obtain an analytic uniform Hartree-Fock saddle point in the lowest Landau level, coherent at every finite separation. Within Hartree-Fock theory, its finite-wave-vector collective-mode instability favors an excitonic supersolid, distinguishing loss of spatial uniformity from loss of coherence. We conjecture that the quantum Hall bilayer evolves continuously toward weak pairing of composite fermions, with a scale far below the mean-field prediction at large separation. This suppression could produce an apparent critical separation at finite temperature without a ground-state quantum phase transition. The comparison separates the existence, energy scale, and spatial stability of interlayer coherence.
