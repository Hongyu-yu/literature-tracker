    纹理化硅晶圆辅助制备可拉伸铂纳米管网络电极及其在柔性压电器件中的应用
    Textured Si Wafer-Assisted Fabrication of Stretchable Platinum Nanotube Network Electrodes for Flexible Piezoelectric Devices
    🎯 核心关注📖 Preprints

    📄 摘要：柔性和可拉伸电极是软电子器件的重要组成部分，这些器件需要在反复机械变形下工作。然而，传统金属薄膜电极通常会因刚性金属层与弹性体基底之间的机械失配而产生裂纹并发生电学失效。在本研究中，我们提出了一种纹理化硅辅助的可拉伸铂纳米管（Pt-NT）网络电极制备策略。以纹理化硅晶圆上的静电纺丝聚乙烯吡咯烷酮（PVP）纳米纤维作为一维牺牲模板，通过溅射铂形成具有受控电连续性的铂包覆纤维网络。增加溅射时间会提高表观铂涂层厚度并降低方块电阻，表明互连铂导电通路逐渐形成。在热去除PVP模板并将其转移至聚二甲基硅氧烷（PDMS）后，Pt-NT电极表现出7.55欧姆/方的低方块电阻。在机械变形下，铂薄膜电极在弯曲过程中出现电学失效，并在拉伸应变超过20%时失效；而Pt-NT电极即使在100%应变下仍能保持导电通路。随后，Pt-NT电极与钛酸钡（BaTiO3）—PDMS压电复合层集成，在25牛顿、3赫兹外力作用下产生约40纳安的输出电流。这些结果表明，纹理化硅辅助的Pt-NT网络电极能够作为柔性压电器件的可变形电极平台。

    💡 亮点：研究提出了一种纹理化硅辅助的可拉伸铂纳米管网络电极制备策略，以静电纺丝聚乙烯吡咯烷酮纳米纤维作为牺牲模板，通过溅射铂形成具有连续导电通路的纤维网络。去除模板并转移至聚二甲基硅氧烷后，电极的方块电阻为7.55欧姆/方，并可承受高达100%的拉伸应变，而传统铂薄膜在弯曲和超过20%的拉伸应变下即发生失效。该电极与钛酸钡—聚二甲基硅氧烷压电复合层集成后，在25牛顿、3赫兹外力下产生约40纳安输出电流，显示出其作为柔性压电器件可变形电极平台的潜力。

    📐 方法要点：这篇的主线是纹理化 Si 晶圆辅助的可拉伸 Pt 纳米管网络电极制备，工艺上以静电纺丝 PVP 纤维为牺牲模板，溅射 Pt 后去模板并转移到 PDMS。它的关键指标很明确：方块电阻约 7.55 Ω/□，可承受 100% 拉伸，应力下仍保持连续导电通路，说明方法重点在几何连通和形貌转移控制。

    🎯 核心关注📖 Machine Learning: Science and Technology

    The rapid urbanization strongly influences the land surface characteristics and aggravates the environmental problems, such as Surface Urban Heat Island (SUHI) effect. The present study focuses on the effect of urban growth pattern on Land Surface Temperature (LST) and precipitation variability in Dera Ghazi Khan, Punjab, Pakistan during 25 years, 2000-2025. The study combines the use of Remote Sensing (RS), Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Google Earth Engine (GEE), and statistical modeling to analyze the temporal and spatial associations of Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) alteration with the thermal environment. The multi-temporal data from Landsat missions and Sentinel-2 datasets was used to categorize the study area into four major classes of LULC namely Water Body, Built-up, Agriculture, and Barren Land. Using a Random Forest machine learning classification model, the LULC map was created for six observation years (2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020 and 2025). Spectral indices such as Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and Normalized Difference Built-up Index (NDBI) were derived to evaluate the vegetation density and urban expansion. The data for Land Surface Temperature was obtained by calculating using thermal infrared bands, and the data of precipitation was obtained from climate datasets included in Google Earth Engine. The Getis-Ord Gi statistic in ArcMap was used for spatial hotspot analysis, while Pearson correlation and linear regression analysis in Microsoft Excel were used to examine the statistical relationships. The outcomes showed significant urban growth for Dera Ghazi Khan during the study period. The extent of built-up land had grown from about 354 Sq. Km in 2000 to nearly 570 Sq. Km in 2025. The area of barren land decreased sharply and the area of Km increased. Results of the analysis showed that high built-up density and high NDBI values were correlated with high LST values. However, vegetation dominated agricultural areas and water bodies showed lower thermal signatures because of the effects of evapotranspiration and thermal inertia. There was also a variation in the average LSTs in the years 2015, with around 49°C, that was attributed to the precipitation variability, where years with higher precipitations also had better vegetation conditions and less thermal stress. The hotspot analysis revealed the spatial growth of thermal hotspots from urban core to periphery. The strong relationship between urban expansion and increase in surface temperature was further verified by statistical analysis. The results show that SUHI effect has been intensified to a great extent in an uncontrolled urbanization phenomenon and has changed the thermal environment of the region of Dera Ghazi Khan. The study offers a valuable scientific foundation for sustainable urban planning and adaptation to climate change in the fast-expanding secondary cities of arid and semi-arid areas. Geographical information systems (GIS) and statistical modeling are powerful tools for urban environmental change monitoring and evidence-based policymaking.
