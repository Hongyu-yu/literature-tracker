    📖 英文原文

    The accuracy of machine learning interatomic potentials suffers from reference data that contain numerical noise. Often originating from unconverged or inconsistent electronic-structure calculations, this noise is challenging to identify. Existing mitigation strategies, such as manual filtering or iterative refinement of outliers, require either substantial expert effort or multiple expensive retraining cycles, making them difficult to scale to large datasets. Here, we introduce a noise-resilient training method that automatically down-weighs noisy samples, without requiring additional reference calculations. By tracking the loss distribution via an exponential moving average, this unsupervised method identifies outliers throughout a single training run. We show that this approach prevents overfitting and matches the performance of iterative refinement baselines with significantly reduced overhead. The method’s effectiveness is demonstrated by recovering accurate physical observables for liquid water from unconverged reference data, including diffusion coefficients. Furthermore, we validate its scalability by training a foundation model for organic chemistry on the SPICE dataset, where it reduces both energy and force errors. This framework provides a simple, automated solution for training robust models on imperfect datasets across dataset sizes.

    📖 英文原文

    Understanding charge storage in layered electrode materials requires distinguishing electrochemical kinetics from the intrinsic ability of the electrode to accumulate electronic charge. Here, phase-pure Bi2O2S was investigated using electrochemical measurements and density functional theory (DFT) calculations to establish this relationship. Cyclic voltammetry, galvanostatic charge-discharge, and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy revealed a strongly rate-dependent charge-storage response, with a specific capacitance of 866 F g⁻¹ at 1 A g⁻¹. The scan-rate dependence gave an average b-value of ~ 0.61, while Dunn analysis showed that diffusion-dependent processes dominate the charge-storage response. DFT calculations further demonstrated that surface modification alters the electronic charge-storage ability of Bi2O2S. The calculated quantum capacitance increased from 493.14 (:μ:Fcm⁻²) for the pristine surface to 638.75 (:μ:Fcm⁻²) following OH adsorption. Surface charge densities of 513.46 and 506.76 (:μ:C:cm⁻²) were obtained for K-top and K-hollow configurations, respectively. Experimental surface charge densities approached the DFT-derived range at higher current densities, whereas larger deviations at lower current densities indicate additional contributions from electrolyte-mediated and Faradaic processes. These results establish an experimental-DFT framework for relating rate-dependent electrochemical charge storage to the intrinsic electronic charge-accumulation behaviour of Bi2O2S.

    The construction of semiconductor heterojunctions combined with elemental doping is a feasible and efficient strategy to optimize the optical properties of photocatalytic materials. In this work, Cu-doped g-C3N4/BiVO4 composite photocatalysts were successfully fabricated via a facile synthetic route. Characterization confirmed that BiVO4 nanoparticles uniformly grow on g-C3N4 nanosheets, with Cu species coordinating with the triazine ring to form a well-contacted heterojunction interface. This work clarifies the structure-activity relationship of modified g-C3N4-based composites via combined experimental and theoretical investigations, providing a reliable reference for the design and preparation of high-efficiency visible-light photocatalysts.

    Hybrid insulin peptide (HIP) fusion junctions are not encoded in the germline proteome and may be absent during thymic negative selection, allowing microbial mimics to encounter T cells that escaped central tolerance. We present a structure-first screening pipeline for HIP mimics in type 1 diabetes, dataset-scoped negative results for motif yield and backbone RMSD, and ten peptides prioritized for experimental testing. Five reference HIP–TCR–MHC systems guided motif construction. The Swiss-Prot screen targeted ten taxa; a contemporary reconstruction contains reviewed sequences for nine. Of 1,092 motif hits, 55 passed predicted HLA-binding and AlphaFold 3 confidence filters and were modelled with TCRModel2 across five stochastic seeds. A composition-preserving shuffled-proteome control showed no aggregate motif-yield enrichment in the contemporary reconstruction (observed/null ratio 0.97). Candidate backbone RMSD had median 0.315 Å and interquartile range 0.274–0.389 Å; the three controls lay inside the candidate distribution at the 21.8th, 61.8th, and 81.8th percentiles. No single RMSD cutoff perfectly separated these sampled groups. A three-metric native-HIP gate using TCR–pMHC ipTM, interface-pLDDT, and ΔG retained 13 candidates; exclusion of three animal-associated Rotavirus A peptides left ten nominees. The nominated set was unchanged by dropping the redundant Kd criterion but was sensitive to the acceptance thresholds. Supporting comparisons found higher ipTM for 48 of 55 candidates, robust to nonparametric testing and multiple-comparison correction, but the candidate-only AlphaFold 3 pre-filter confounds that separation. RRNVATLQAENVTG from SARS-CoV-2 and SDLLKSVDSEEVRE from human rhinovirus 3 are co-priorities undergoing experimental testing: confidence and directional agreement favor the former, whereas interface energetics favor the latter. Their relative ordering is not statistically established. The pipeline supplies testable peptide–TCR–MHC combinations and documents the limits of motif-yield and backbone-similarity filters in these analyses.
