    📖 英文原文

    Learning potential energy surfaces for magnetic materials requires resolving coupled local spin manifolds and geometric frustration. We introduce SCO-Hyper-Meta-Double-GroupNN, a geometry-only architecture combining a pretrained O(3)-equivariant backbone with SU(2) spinor Hamiltonians and cooperative hyper-/meta-graph interactions. Analytic thermal spinor density matrices provide non-polynomial two- and three-body structural invariants, while frustrated-triangle hyperedges and a multi-shell sideway gate couple geometric representations to local spin manifolds. A variationally consistent, doubly-centered zero-gauge free-energy functional couples multiplet occupations and signed spin orientations, yielding conservative autograd forces without spin inputs at inference. On a first-principles Co-Cr-Ni benchmark comprising 346 training and 59 held-out validation supercells across crystalline, amorphous, glassy, and liquid regimes, compact models with 56k-77k parameters outperform a 207k-parameter spin-conditioned magnetic MACE baseline. Energy MAE decreases by 37-44%, reaching 3.82 versus 6.83 meV/atom, and force MAE decreases by 10.8-14.2 meV/Angstrom, reaching 165.8-169.2 versus 180.0 meV/Angstrom. The models preserve Kramers degeneracy to within 6×10⁻¹⁵ eV, predict equilibrium local magnetic moments from geometry alone with an absolute-moment MAE of 0.181,μ_B, and support stable molecular dynamics up to 2,200 K. This framework integrates local spinor symmetry and cooperative magnetic interactions into geometry-only interatomic potentials.

    作者：Rui Ding、Zixin Ding、Rodrigo Pires Ferreira、Diantong Li、Junhong Chen、Yuxin Chen。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-26

    Pretrained universal machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) are widely expected to substitute for explicit quantum chemistry when screening molecular interactions. On non-covalent binding we find that this expectation holds for neutral chemistry and fails for charged chemistry. We audit the FAIRChem UMA OMol head, without task-specific fine-tuning, on 5,567 probe-target dimers from 18 benchmarks across nine fidelity arms, using RAPIDS (Rapid Atomistic Probe-target Interaction Discovery Scaffold), a training-free inference-time scaffold. Third, the residual failures are structured: signed biases cluster by chemistry family, and whether a fidelity fails catastrophically transfers across families more reliably than error magnitude, enabling cost-aware fidelity selection. RAPIDS-Select, a closed-form rule on cheap precomputed features, uses about 37% less compute than a strong static DFT baseline at matched accuracy, and on a newly computed charged benchmark cuts the catastrophe rate from 68% to 5.7%. On WetSenseBench, a wet-lab-grounded host-guest selectivity benchmark from eight sensing studies, RAPIDS without DFT follow-up recovers 82.9% pair-level agreement on the seven studies above its ~1 kcal/mol precision floor.

    Large-scale pretrained machine-learned interatomic potentials keep growing more accurate as new models appear at a rapid pace, and more expensive: the current leader of Matbench Discovery as of November 2026 (F1 score 0.935) has 9.9×10⁷ parameters and took 3.6 thousand GPU-hours to train.     Foundation interatomic potentials capture structural disorder in metallic glasses#
    作者：Dario Massa、Luca Leoni、Antoni Wadowski、Kacper Cybinski、Anshul D. S. Parmar、Carmine Autieri 等 10 人。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-26
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×化学·材料AI×科学交叉6方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：基础机器学习原子间势能在化学和结构空间之间很好地传递，但其对于高度无序金属态的可靠性尚不明确。金属玻璃是一个要求很高的基准：传统上，它们是使用适合特定成分的嵌入式原子方法（EAM）势来建模的，而从头算分子动力学在所需的尺寸和时间尺度上仍然过于昂贵。使用原型 Cu-Zr-Al 玻璃，我们表明基础电势可以捕获结构无序，以接近第一原理的精度再现非晶态的结构、淬火动力学以及弹性、机械和热性能。我们将 MACE 基础模型与沿分子动力学淬火的经典 EAM 势进行基准测试，根据密度泛函理论和实验进行验证，首先在 Cu46Zr46Al8 处，然后穿过三元材料的良好玻璃形成区域。我们进一步测试了两条专业化路线：对使用 Lennard-Jones 代理模型生成的玻璃结构进行微调，以及对自动生成的小周期单元 (ASSYST) 进行微调。在包含非平衡液体和无定形配置（例如 OMAT）的数据库上预先训练的模型是准确的，并且可以开箱即用地跨成分转移，明显优于特定成分的 EAM 潜力。对目标玻璃结构的微调只能带来适度的增益，而对小晶体细胞的专业化会降低对无序的描述。因此，无序金属的基础潜力的准备情况主要取决于其预训练数据的多样性，而不是通过特定于系统的微调，将建模工作从定制数据集生成转移到预训练覆盖和对所得动态的仔细验证。

    📖 英文原文

    Foundation machine-learning interatomic potentials transfer well across chemical and structural space, yet their reliability for highly disordered metallic states is less established. Metallic glasses are a demanding benchmark: they have traditionally been modeled with Embedded Atom Method (EAM) potentials fitted to specific compositions, while ab initio molecular dynamics remains too expensive at the required sizes and timescales. Using the prototypical Cu-Zr-Al glass, we show that foundation potentials capture structural disorder, reproducing the structure, quenching dynamics, and elastic, mechanical and thermal properties of the amorphous state at near first-principles accuracy. We benchmark MACE foundation models against a classical EAM potential along molecular dynamics quenches, validating against density functional theory and experiment, first at Cu46Zr46Al8 and then across the good glass-forming region of the ternary. We further test two specialization routes: fine-tuning on glass structures generated with Lennard-Jones surrogate models, and on automatically generated small periodic cells (ASSYST). Models pre-trained on databases that include non-equilibrium liquid and amorphous configurations, such as OMAT, are accurate and transferable across composition out of the box, clearly outperforming the composition-specific EAM potential. Fine-tuning on target glass structures brings only modest gains, while specialization on small crystalline cells degrades the description of disorder. The readiness of foundation potentials for disordered metals is therefore set mainly by the diversity of their pre-training data rather than by system-specific fine-tuning, shifting the modeling effort from bespoke dataset generation to pre-training coverage and careful validation of the resulting dynamics.
