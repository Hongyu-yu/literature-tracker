    高效的机器学习原子间势#
    Efficient Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials#
    作者：Yury Lysogorskiy、Anton Bochkarev、Ralf Drautz。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-18
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：机器学习原子间势 (MLIP) 的精度接近量子力学参考计算，而成本仅为其一小部分。效率是多层投资计划的目的，但高效多层投资计划的策略尚未得到系统的审查。本次审查以效率为指导方针。为了在共同基础上比较 MLIP，我们用完整的基础扩展、原子簇扩展 (ACE) 及其图泛化 (GRACE) 来表示它们。该展开式是规范的，由单粒子函数的乘积构建。经典的原子间势（例如嵌入原子方法）和许多现代 MLIP（包括等变变压器）可以在此展开中表示。因此，MLIP 架构的许多明显多样性反映了如何处理非线性、层耦合和优化，而不是模型可以表示哪些交互。我们展示了 MLIP 的成本如何在系统规模上以及在张量分解的固定秩下在模型复杂度上变得线性，并且我们讨论了机器学习的作用，它提供了非线性参数化和优化。由于效率仅在给定精度下才有意义，因此我们还比较了精度和成本。在 Matbench Discovery 排行榜上，三到五层的模型接近更深层次模型的精度，而一个基础（沿着误差和速度的 Pareto 前沿截断）的成本跨越两个数量级以上。最终，训练数据决定了任何 MLIP 的质量。

    📖 英文原文

    Machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) reach an accuracy close to quantum mechanical reference calculations at a small fraction of the cost. Efficiency is the purpose of MLIPs, yet strategies for efficient MLIPs have not been reviewed systematically. This review takes efficiency as its guideline. To compare MLIPs on a common footing, we represent them in a complete basis expansion, the Atomic Cluster Expansion (ACE) and its graph generalization (GRACE). The expansion is canonical, built from products of single-particle functions. Classical interatomic potentials, such as the embedded-atom method, and many modern MLIPs, including equivariant transformers, can be represented within this expansion. Much of the apparent diversity of MLIP architectures therefore reflects how non-linearity, layer coupling and optimization are handled rather than which interactions the models can represent. We show how the cost of an MLIP becomes linear in system size and, at fixed rank of the tensor decomposition, in model complexity, and we discuss the role of machine learning, which provides non-linear parameterizations and optimization. Because efficiency is meaningful only at a given accuracy, we also compare accuracy and cost. On the Matbench Discovery leaderboard, models with three to five layers approach the accuracy of deeper models, and one basis, truncated along the Pareto front of error and speed, spans more than two orders of magnitude in cost. Ultimately, training data determine the quality of any MLIP.

    作者：Bohan Zhang、Zibo Wu、Yunzhi Li、Wei-Hai Fang、Zhendong Li。arXiv · physics，2026-11-18
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配3

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：基于分子基态神经网络量子态 (NQS) 的成功，自然激发态 (NES) 等多态框架 [Pfau et al., Science 2024, 385, eadn0137] 将 NQS 扩展到化学上重要的激发态。然而，如果不控制总自旋，此类计算可能需要包含其他自旋的许多干预状态，特别是在具有密集低能谱的强相关系统中。因此，我们概括了之前开发的自旋适应神经网络回流（SA-NNBF）ansatz [Li et al., arXiv:2604.06841 (2026)]，以提高其在指定自旋扇区内激发态计算的变分灵活性。我们在选定的轨道空间和自旋扇区内建立了这种扩展 ansatz 的普遍性，并使用数值基准来评估参数分配如何影响 ansatz 的准确性。借助自旋适应 ansatz，我们能够直接优化 O2 的第一激发态（单线态），而无需使用任何多态框架。将扩展 ansatz 与 NES 相结合，我们获得了 O2 的低位三重态和 [Fe2S2(SCH3)4]2- 团簇的单重态，而不包括其他自旋的介入态。单态和 NES 计算都实现了相对于精确能量的化学精度。我们的工作提供了一个实用的 NQS 框架，用于针对强相关系统中指定自旋的激发态。

    📖 英文原文

    Building on the success of neural-network quantum states (NQS) for molecular ground states, multi-state frameworks such as natural excited states (NES) [Pfau et al., Science 2024, 385, eadn0137] extend NQS to chemically important excited states. However, without control over total spin, such calculations can require the inclusion of many intervening states of other spins, particularly in strongly correlated systems with dense low-energy spectra. We therefore generalize our previously developed spin-adapted neural network backflow (SA-NNBF) ansatz [Li et al., arXiv:2604.06841 (2026)] to improve its variational flexibility for excited-state calculations within a prescribed spin sector. We establish the universality of this extended ansatz within the chosen orbital space and spin sector, and use numerical benchmarks to assess how parameter allocation affects the ansatz's accuracy. With the spin-adapted ansatz, we are able to directly optimize the first excited state (a singlet) of O₂ without using any multi-state frameworks. Combining the extended ansatz with NES, we obtain low-lying triplet states of O₂ and singlet states of the [Fe₂S₂(SCH₃)₄]²⁻ cluster without including intervening states of other spins. Both the single-state and NES calculations achieve chemical accuracy relative to the exact energies. Our work provides a practical NQS framework for targeting excited states of prescribed spin in strongly correlated systems.

    Stein Kernelized Molecular Dynamics for Active Learning of Interatomic Potentials#
    作者：Joanna Zou、Fraser Birks、Dallas Foster、Youssef Marzouk。arXiv · physics，2026-11-18
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📖 英文原文

    Machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) enable efficient and accurate atomistic simulations but depend critically on the quality and diversity of the training data. We introduce Stein kernelized molecular dynamics (SKMD), an enhanced sampling method that uses interacting particle dynamics to acquire informative training configurations for the fine-tuning of MLIPs. SKMD corresponds to a stochastic variant of Stein variational gradient descent that is adapted for molecular dynamics by incorporating asynchronous particle updates and a kernel of global atomic descriptors, which provides a symmetry-aware measure of configurational similarity. Unlike other enhanced samplers used in molecular dynamics, SKMD maintains the Boltzmann distribution as a stationary distribution of its dynamics in the appropriate asymptotic limit. This property enforces a balance between the exploration of diverse configurations and attraction toward high-probability regions of the energy landscape. We further propose an approach to efficient online data acquisition using an adaptive stopping criterion that selects non-redundant training data over the course of simulation. We demonstrate SKMD for the active learning of a neural network model of the Müller--Brown potential, a MACE interatomic potential for alanine dipeptide, and a MACE foundation model with a DFT oracle for predicting the Pandey reconstruction of the Si(111) surface. Compared to active learning baselines, our method achieves higher model accuracy in fewer training iterations.

    使用经典和机器学习的原子间势研究 LiF 中的动力学和传输#
    Dynamics and transport in LiF using classical and machine learned interatomic potentials#
    作者：Harvey L. Devereux、Margaret-Ann Withington、Alin M. Elena、Cillian Cockrell、Kostya Trachenko。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-18
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：我们对熔融氟化锂进行分子动力学模拟，将经典的 Buckingham 和 Born-Huggins-Mayer 势与机器学习的原子间势 - MACE-MP-0b3（小）基础模型势及其微调版本进行比较。我们发现，开箱即用的 MACE-MP-0b3 能够准确地再现与可极化电势相当的液态实验剪切粘度，尽管它本身不是可极化的。以前使用经典势计算的粘度预测不足，这归因于熔化温度受到抑制。受此启发，我们比较了这些电势的熔点。我们发现机器学习势解决了经典势熔点的显着低估问题，尽管精炼势和基础模型之间存在显着差异。我们探讨了每个模型的微观结构和动力学，以进一步阐明经典势和机器学习势之间粘度的显着变化。我们发现，尽管它们的熔化温度较低，但经典势由于其固定电荷而产生相当僵硬、频率更高的单粒子和集体电荷传输动力学。然而，在长波长限制下，多体机器学习势具有更具弹性的集体运动，它控制动量的扩散，因此导致剪切粘度增强。

    📖 英文原文

    We perform molecular dynamics simulations of molten Lithium Fluoride, comparing the classical Buckingham and Born-Huggins-Mayer potentials with machine-learnt interatomic potentials - the MACE-MP-0b3 (small) foundation model potential, and a fine-tuned version thereof. We find that the MACE-MP-0b3, out-of-the-box, is able to accurately reproduce the experimental shear viscosity across the liquid state comparable with polarisable potentials despite not itself being polarisable. Previous viscosities computed using classical potentials are under-predicted, which has previously been attributed to a suppressed melting temperature. Prompted by this we compare the melting points of these potentials. We find that the machine-learnt potentials address the significant underestimation of the melting point of the classical potentials, though the refined potential and the foundation model differ significantly between themselves. We probe the microscopic structure and dynamics of each model to further elucidate the considerable change in viscosity between classical and machine-learnt potentials. We find that, despite their lower melting temperature, classical potentials produce considerably stiffer, higher frequency, single-particle and collective charge-transport dynamics on account of their fixed charges. However, in the long-wavelength limit, the many-body machine-learnt potentials feature more elastic collective motion that governs the diffusion of momentum and is therefore responsible for the enhanced shear viscosity.

    张量积路径何时重要？等变原子间势的群论分析#
    When Do Tensor Product Paths Matter? A Group-Theoretic Analysis of Equivariant Interatomic Potentials#
    作者：Jiawei Wang、Sai Gu、Laurence T. Yang、Junfu Tan、Yemin Yu、Ziheng Lu。arXiv · physics，2026-11-18
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉8方向匹配6

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：等变原子间势中的张量积 (TP) 层通过允许对称的路径对角度相互作用进行编码。然而，本地站点对称性可能会导致允许的路径在邻居聚合后不承载任何信号。我们称这种效应为聚合取消。在标量输入第一层中，完整位点对称轨道上的同种邻居共享标量和径向因子，从而减少了它们对球谐轨道和的角度贡献。如果受限的 l 度表示没有微不足道的分量，则对于任何学习的径向权重，该总和以及相应的路径都会消失。对于理想的 Oₕ 八面体轨道，这会删除 l_edge=1,2,3 类。在第一层之后，因式分解不再成立，因此不再保证取消；相反，我们要问的是，平均而言，受影响的路径是否仍然不那么重要。我们在 NequIP 中分别测试这两个水平：匹配半径 TiO₆ 探针检查第一层抵消，而模型内 CaTiO₃ 比较和六个 OMat24 模型检查重要性偏差。然后，我们对训练帧上的路径进行排名，在验证帧上选择修剪分数，并在保留测试中评估冻结掩模。在最高评估分数（174 条路径中的 60%）下，CaTiO₃、OMat24 Oₕ-立方和 OMat24 低对称性数据的力平均绝对误差 (MAE) 变化分别为 1.42%、0.04% 和 1.83%。这些掩模意味着 TP 操作潜在减少 67.12-71.94%，边缘多层感知器 (MLP) 潜在减少 59.77%；这些是结构代理，而不是测量的加速。结果将局部对称性与 NequIP 中的路径冗余和修剪鲁棒性联系起来；转移到其他架构仍然开放。

    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：interatomic potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Tensor-product (TP) layers in equivariant interatomic potentials encode angular interactions through symmetry-allowed paths. Yet local site symmetry can make an allowed path carry no signal after neighbor aggregation. We call this effect Aggregation Cancellation. In a scalar-input first layer, equal-species neighbors on a complete site-symmetry orbit share scalar and radial factors, reducing their angular contribution to a spherical-harmonic orbit sum. If the restricted degree-l representation has no trivial component, this sum and the corresponding path vanish for any learned radial weight. For an ideal Oₕ octahedral orbit, this removes the l_edge=1,2,3 classes. Beyond the first layer, the factorisation no longer holds, so cancellation is no longer guaranteed; we instead ask whether affected paths remain less important on average. We test the two levels separately in NequIP: matched-radius TiO₆ probes examine first-layer cancellation, whereas a within-model CaTiO₃ comparison and six OMat24 models examine the importance bias. We then rank paths on training frames, choose the pruning fraction on validation frames, and evaluate frozen masks on held-out tests. At the highest evaluated fraction (60% of 174 paths), force mean absolute error (MAE) changes are 1.42%, 0.04%, and 1.83% for CaTiO₃, OMat24 Oₕ-cubic, and OMat24 low-symmetry data, respectively. The masks imply 67.12-71.94% potential TP-operation reduction and 59.77% potential edge multilayer perceptron (MLP) reduction; these are structural proxies, not measured speedups. The results connect local symmetry to path redundancy and pruning robustness in NequIP; transfer to other architectures remains open.

    We demonstrate the feasibility of predicting the lineshape of an electronic molecular transition collisionally perturbed by another molecule from first principles of quantum mechanics. We consider an N₂-perturbed O₂ A-band transition, which is relevant to spectroscopic observations in Earth's atmosphere. This approach simultaneously incorporates the electronic structure, quantum scattering and lineshape effects required to accurately predict pressure- and temperature-dependence of observed lineshapes. The ab initio lineshapes reproduce measurements to better than 0.6%. Theory also provides new physical constraints on beyond-Voigt lineshape parameters in fits of high-precision data, yielding fit residuals at the 0.08% level. This work motivates advances in molecular electronic structure calculations and demonstrates the potential to generate comprehensive spectroscopic reference data from first principles.
