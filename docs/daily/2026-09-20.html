    🧭 材料 / 器件P3 · 其他📖 Preprints👤 Sonu Choudhary, Surojit Konar, Yogheshwar Mishra, Sharad Tiwari🔥 AI 6

    📖 英文原文

    Monitoring coal-mine rehabilitation primarily relies on single vegetation indices, which reflect greenness but overlook surface transitions that precede vegetation growth, and are rarely integrated into a reproducible spatio-temporal workflow. Surface albedo is a promising yet underutilized metric for early-stage reclamation, but integrated multi-indicator assessments remain sparse. This study addresses this gap for the Amrapali opencast coal-mine overburden in Chatra, Jharkhand, establishing a pre-restoration baseline (2013–2026). Annual composites of surface albedo, land surface temperature (LST) and NDVI were derived from Landsat imagery across a 10 km buffer, and land use/land cover (LULC) change was quantified (2015–2025). Trends were assessed with OLS regression, the Mann–Kendall test and Sen's slope, and relationships with Pearson and Spearman correlations. Surface albedo showed a significant declining trend (Mann–Kendall p = 0.004; Sen's slope = − 0.00137 yr⁻¹; R² = 0.60), the clearest signal of landscape change, consistent with progressive darkening of the stabilizing overburden. NDVI showed no significant trend but high variability (CV = 13.4%), and LST remained stable despite an approximately 3.7-fold mine expansion. A significant inverse NDVI–LST relationship (Spearman ρ = −0.55, p = 0.043) confirmed vegetation cooling, whereas weak albedo–LST and NDVI–albedo associations indicate that surface material changes, not canopy greenness, governed the albedo decline. LULC analysis documented contraction of open land ( ~ − 39%) and vegetation ( ~ − 16%) alongside mine, built-up and agricultural expansion. By fusing albedo, LST, NDVI and LULC in a spatially-explicit, multi-temporal framework built on openly available data, this study identifies albedo as a sensitive early-stage indicator and contributes a transparent, transferable geospatial workflow and baseline for evaluating future restoration.

    🧭 材料 / 器件P3 · 其他📖 Preprints👤 Xiaoyan Wang, Qingru Chen, Wenxin Lin, Yue Gao, Yunyi Qin, Dawei Gao 等8位作者🔥 AI 6

    📖 英文原文

    Neonatal incubators may serve as environmental reservoirs for antimicrobial-resistant bacteria in neonatal intensive care units. In this study, Staphylococcus epidermidis HE23 was isolated from a high-touch surface of a neonatal incubator and characterized using whole-genome sequencing, comparative genomics, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, and metabolomic analysis. Hybrid assembly of Illumina and Nanopore sequencing reads generated a complete genome consisting of a circular chromosome (2,542,743 bp) and two plasmids (50,114 bp and 9,031 bp). Genomic analysis identified nine antibiotic resistance genes, including the chromosomal β-lactam resistance determinants blaZ and mecA and the plasmid-borne mupirocin resistance gene mupA. Antimicrobial susceptibility testing further confirmed resistance to multiple antibiotics, including β-lactams and quinolones. Genes associated with resistance to cationic antimicrobial peptides were also identified, suggesting potential mechanisms that may facilitate persistence under antimicrobial pressure. Metabolomic profiling detected several secondary metabolites, including putatively annotated cinobufagin and harmaline. Comparative genomic analysis further revealed conserved and strain-specific genomic features of HE23. The coexistence of chromosomal resistance determinants and plasmid-associated mupA highlights the potential of incubator-associated S. epidermidis to act as a reservoir of antimicrobial resistance in neonatal intensive care environments. These findings support strengthened microbial surveillance and targeted infection-control strategies for neonatal incubators.
