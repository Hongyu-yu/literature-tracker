    作者：Ekaterina Spirande、Rustam Arabov、Sofya Demina、Nikita Rybin、Alexander Shapeev。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-10-23

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：Rb2SO4、Rb2Ca2(SO4)3 和 Ba3Lu(BO3)3 是结构复杂的材料，其热膨胀已在高温 X 射线衍射的基础上根据特定的多面体重排进行了解释。我们使用分子动力学研究它们的晶格动力学及其对热和电子性质的影响，并使用密度泛函理论数据训练的主动学习矩张量势。对于 β-Rb2SO4、Rb2Ca2(SO4)3 和 Ba₃Lu(BO3)3，非谐性测度 σ^A（将原子间作用力的非谐性贡献与作用在原子上的总力进行比较）达到约 0.3-0.5，对于取向无序的 α-Rb2SO4，非谐性测度约为 0.75，虚部谐波模式为Rb2Ca2(SO4)3和α-Rb2SO4在有限温度下稳定。模拟再现了测得的热膨胀并支持衍射提出的机制：β-Rb2SO4中沿c的膨胀是由波纹Rb链的伸长和拉直引起的，BO3重新取向伴随着Ba3Lu(BO3)3的各向异性膨胀，多面体连接的弯曲限制了沿弱膨胀方向和立方体Rb2Ca2(SO4)3的膨胀。 Green-Kubo 晶格热导率在具有可比 σ^A 的化合物之间相差超过一个数量级，这表明非谐性测量本身并不能决定热传输。声子引起的带隙重整化（包括晶格热膨胀）在 Rb2Ca2(SO4)3 和 Ba3Lu(BO3)3 中在 1250 K 下达到 2.7 和 1.7 eV，在 β-Rb2SO4 中在 650 K 下达到 1.4 eV，零点重整化分别为 0.64、0.70 和β-Rb2SO4、Rb2Ca2(SO4)3 和 Ba3Lu(BO3)3 分别为 0.31 eV。

    Rb₂SO₄, Rb₂Ca₂(SO₄)₃, and Ba₃Lu(BO₃)₃ are structurally complex materials whose thermal expansion has been interpreted, on the basis of high-temperature x-ray diffraction, in terms of specific polyhedral rearrangements. We study their lattice dynamics and its consequences for thermal and electronic properties using molecular dynamics with actively learned moment tensor potentials trained on density-functional theory data. The anharmonicity measure σ^A (compares the anharmonic contribution to the interatomic forces with the total forces acting on atoms) reaches approx0.3-0.5 for β-Rb₂SO₄, Rb₂Ca₂(SO₄)₃, and Ba₃Lu(BO₃)₃ and approx0.75 for orientationally disordered α-Rb₂SO₄, and the imaginary harmonic modes of Rb₂Ca₂(SO₄)₃ and α-Rb₂SO₄ are stabilized at finite temperature. The simulations reproduce the measured thermal expansion and support the mechanisms proposed from diffraction: expansion along c in β-Rb₂SO₄ arises from both elongation and straightening of corrugated Rb chains, BO₃ reorientation accompanies the anisotropic expansion of Ba₃Lu(BO₃)₃, and bending of polyhedral linkages limits expansion along the weakly expanding directions and in cubic Rb₂Ca₂(SO₄)₃. Green-Kubo lattice thermal conductivities differ by more than an order of magnitude between compounds with comparable σ^A, showing that the anharmonicity measure alone does not determine heat transport. Phonon-induced band-gap renormalization, including lattice thermal expansion, reaches 2.7 and 1.7 eV at 1250 K in Rb₂Ca₂(SO₄)₃ and Ba₃Lu(BO₃)₃ and 1.4 eV at 650 K in β-Rb₂SO₄, with zero-point renormalizations of 0.64, 0.70, and 0.31 eV for β-Rb₂SO₄, Rb₂Ca₂(SO₄)₃, and Ba₃Lu(BO₃)₃, respectively.

    Universal machine-learned interatomic potentials (uMLIPs) are ranked by their formation-energy error against a DFT database, as if that database were ground truth, but independent DFT databases disagree. We benchmark fourteen uMLIPs on 1.45 million ordered intermetallics against AFLOW, which none trained on, and compare every energy and stability error with that disagreement. For half the structures, AFLOW, Alexandria, and OQMD differ by less than 6-8 meV/atom, but a minority differ by far more, raising the average to 16-30. Once broken elemental references are screened, the energy and stability rankings agree almost everywhere. Three apparent model failures shrink or vanish under the right control: poorly correlated shear moduli, thousands of "bad" DFT references, and the leader's inflated lead.

    📖 英文原文

    Predicting speciation in concentrated electrolytes requires accurate ion interactions and adequate sampling of slowly interconverting coordination environments. We combine Zn K-edge extended X-ray absorption fine structure (EXAFS) measurements with long-timescale molecular dynamics (MD) using machine-learning interatomic potentials to study aqueous ZnCl2 from dilute conditions to 30 m. We train revPBE-based DeepMD models with D3 corrections included or excluded for Zn-containing pairs and compare them with DPMD-SCAN, classical MD (CMD), and MACE-MATPES-r2SCAN. EXAFS calculations are benchmarked against hydrated and chloride-coordinated Zn reference complexes, then applied to MD ensembles alongside Zn-Cl coordination free-energy analysis. Increasing concentration reduces octahedral coordination, replaces water with chloride, and promotes chloride-bridged association. All three DeepMD models yield similar EXAFS at 3 m, but DPMD-SCAN shows greater transferability across concentrations, reproducing measured spectral trends, key features of mixed ZnCl2/LiCl spectra, and octahedral fractions consistent with other studies. DFT treatment strongly affects coordination free energies and aggregation; excluding Zn-related D3 corrections does not systematically improve EXAFS agreement. CMD shows larger spectral discrepancies and less consistent coordination trends. MACE-MATPES-r2SCAN improves upon CMD and yields spectra comparable to DPMD-SCAN, which provides more consistent agreement and greater computational efficiency. Neighbor-resolved analysis shows that distance disorder and scattering-path interference can produce similar EXAFS signatures for distinct coordination ensembles, highlighting the need for rigorous sampling and ensemble-averaged spectra to distinguish isolated from chloride-bridged Zn species.

    Unconstrained learning of equivariant atomistic properties#

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：graph neural networks、interatomic potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Machine-learned interatomic potentials based on graph neural networks (GNNs) have made first-principles accuracy routine for the potential energy surface (PES). Their backbones, often O(3)-equivariant by design, are increasingly used to learn other atomistic properties. Many of these are of high angular order or pseudotensorial character, defined per atom, or expanded on large atom-centered basis sets. Such targets strain equivariant GNNs, whose tensor products grow steeply in cost with angular order and can fail to represent the target if truncated, forcing difficult trade-offs between hyperparameters. Unconstrained models like the Point-Edge Transformer (PET) instead replace explicit O(3) algebra with edge-to-edge attention and learn equivariance from data, so that the same backbone can in principle learn any equivariant property. We demonstrate how to use PET to learn molecular dipoles and polarizabilities, NMR chemical shielding tensors, and auxiliary-basis expansions of the electron density. Increasing the backbone size systematically improves both accuracy and equivariance error, and targeted architectural and training choices reduce the symmetry breaking further. PET matches or improves on the released reference models at lower inference cost and, compared with equivariant models, a smaller memory footprint. The predictions give accurate IR and Raman spectra and isotropic shieldings; the predicted densities accelerate self-consistent field calculations and give one-shot ground-state properties, and pretrained density models transfer to higher levels of theory by fine-tuning on little data. The impact of approximate equivariance is small for most observables, further validating this approach to train fast, accurate, and useful surrogate models for properties beyond the PES.

    We discuss phases of interacting fermionic Luttinger liquids without particle number conservation, such as those in proximity to a superconductor, subjected to weak continuous measurements of their particle density, in the regime where the bosonization approach is applicable. We show that the phases of these systems are controlled by the competition between measurements, which favor the area-law phase and particle tunneling in and out of the Luttinger liquid, which favors the volume-law phase. The volume-law phase in this system is always charge fuzzy. We show that within this framework, the phase boundary between the two phases is given by a universal dependence of the Luttinger parameter on the measurement rate. This behavior can be contrasted with the non-universal phase boundaries in more generic highly excited one dimensional systems whose behavior is no longer captured by the Luttinger liquid framework.
