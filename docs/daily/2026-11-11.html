    作者：Zain Ul Abideen、Mario Fernández-Pendás、Muhammad Usman、Camilo Rodríguez Quintero、Masuma Suleymanova、Ignacio Arganda-Carreras 等 7 人。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-11-11

    Machine-learning force fields enable atomistic simulations at near-reference accuracy and substantially reduced computational cost, but their development remains fragmented across software frameworks with incompatible data formats, configuration schemes, dependencies, and execution procedures. Orchestr.AI addresses this limitation through a unified workflow that coordinates different MLIP software frameworks, referred to as engines, for data preparation, model training, fine-tuning, evaluation, and deployment. Beyond training, Orchestr.AI provides modular architectural interfaces that connect trained models to ASE-based simulations, batch inference, epistemic uncertainty estimation, calibration, and active-learning workflows. The platform therefore provides a reproducible and extensible infrastructure for developing, comparing, and deploying machine-learning interatomic potentials, which we validate here through cross-engine accuracy and repeatability benchmarks.

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：machine learning potential、density functional theory、interatomic potential、molecular dynamics。

    📖 英文原文

    Atomistic simulations of electrochemical interfaces typically require large system sizes and long simulation times to obtain reliable thermodynamic properties. In this context, machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) allow for computationally efficient sampling compared to density functional theory-based molecular dynamics simulations. Although conventional short-range MLIPs are recognized as inadequate for modeling the (potentially nanometer-wide) diffuse layer, they may still be capable of describing the inner electric double layer that is relevant for electrochemical reactions. Here, we evaluate the performance of common (short-ranged) MLIP architectures [the deep potential DP, the (graph) atomic cluster expansion-based models GRACE and MACE, and the pre-trained eSEN-OC25] for charged Au/electrolyte interfaces. In the evaluation, we focus on the effect of message passing and on the potentially adverse effect of data interference when training for a broad range of surface charges. However, when trained on datasets that span multiple charge states, the MLIPs tested yield inconsistent predictions of interfacial water orientation and ion distributions. This behavior can be traced to the MLIPs being “blind” to the actual surface charge. Message-passing models show somewhat greater robustness to mixed-charge training data, likely due to their larger receptive field, but are still prone to artifacts and errors in the description of the interfacial structure. Overall, our results provide practical guidance on the expected accuracy and cross-charge-state transferability of short-ranged MLIPs for the description of the inner layer of electrode/electrolyte interfaces that will be relevant to their use in computational electrocatalysis.

    PINNverse 遇上乔丹——维格纳：学习长自旋链的哈密顿量#
    PINNverse meets Jordan--Wigner: learning the Hamiltonian of long spin chains#
    作者：Gubio G De Lima。arXiv · physics，2026-11-11
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉6方向匹配3

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：逆物理信息神经网络 (PINNverse) 从测量的期望值中学习量子哈密顿量的参数，但网络输出随着量子位数量 N 的增加而增长为 4^N。我们表明，对于具有最近邻交换相互作用的自旋链，Jordan-Wigner 变换消除了这种指数成本：动力学在 N×N 单体密度矩阵上闭合，因此网络只需要 N² 输出，并且数据是在五个全局设置中测量的局部可观测量。寄生 ZZ 耦合（串扰）通过时间相关的 Hartree--Fock 闭包包含在内。利用合成数据，同一网络首先求解运动方程，然后恢复最多 50 个量子位的链的哈密顿参数，学习到的耦合优于 0.1%。通过从网络估计开始的直接最小二乘拟合来消除长链的局部频率中的误差。

    📖 英文原文

    Inverse physics-informed neural networks (PINNverse) learn the parameters of a quantum Hamiltonian from measured expectation values, but the network output grows as 4^N with the number of qubits N. We show that, for spin chains with nearest-neighbor exchange interactions, the Jordan-Wigner transformation removes this exponential cost: the dynamics closes on an N× N one-body density matrix, so the network needs only N² outputs and the data are local observables measured in five global settings. Parasitic ZZ couplings (crosstalk) are included through a time-dependent Hartree--Fock closure. With synthetic data, the same network first solves the equation of motion and then recovers the Hamiltonian parameters of chains of up to 50 qubits, with couplings learned to better than 0.1%. Errors in the local frequencies of long chains are removed by a direct least-squares fit started from the network estimate.

    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：solid-state electrolytes、crystal structure prediction、interatomic potential、molecular dynamics。

    📖 英文原文

    Mixed-anion lithium oxychlorides exhibit high Li conductivity, but the relationship between oxygen content, structural framework, and Li-ion transport remains unclear. Here, crystal structure prediction and molecular dynamics simulations with machine-learning interatomic potentials are used to examine Li_yMOₓCl₆₋₂ₓ (M = Nb and Ta [Group 5] and M = Ti, Zr, and Hf [Group 4]). Increasing oxygen content changes isolated chloride-rich polyhedra into one-dimensional chains and subsequently more highly connected two- and three-dimensional frameworks. In the Group 5 series, fast Li diffusion occurs over x approx 0.67--1.5, where the Li sites form a three-dimensional percolating network. Potential of mean force maps and nudged elastic band calculations reveal that fast Li-ion transport requires a low-dimensional framework supporting long-range migration through undercoordinated Li sites with low barriers, together with similar energies across Li sites. Finally, we perform fine-grid compositional search, which identifies LiNbO₀.₆₇Cl₄.₆₇, LiTaO₀.₇₅Cl₄.₅, LiNbO₁.₅Cl₃, and LiTaO₁.₅Cl₃ as stable candidates with predicted room-temperature self-diffusivities of up to sim 10⁻⁶,cm²/s. These findings establish how oxygen content controls framework and Li-ion transport, guiding the design of lithium oxyhalide electrolytes.

    We report direction-selective anomalous quantum transport in epitaxial YbSb₂ thin films associated with an interplay between anisotropic topological surface states and an electronic instability. Quantum oscillations reveal a quasi-two-dimensional orbit with a non-trivial Berry phase and Dirac-like dispersion, observable for current along the a-axis but absent along the orthogonal in-plane c-axis. Together with first-principles calculations, these observations support its assignment to a saddle-like topological surface state. Its oscillation amplitude exhibits a field-dependent, non-monotonic temperature evolution that departs from conventional Lifshitz-Kosevich behavior. Raman and coherent phonon spectroscopy reveal additional low-temperature modes consistent with a density-wave instability. Their pronounced polarization anisotropy and colossal field-induced Raman enhancement suggest a strongly anisotropic, field-enhanced electronic susceptibility associated with the density-wave modes. Our findings point to dynamical coupling between anisotropic topological surface states and a field-responsive density-wave instability as a route to direction-selective anomalous quantum transport.
