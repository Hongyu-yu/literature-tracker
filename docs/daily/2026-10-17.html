  1. 05
    La2SmNi2O7 中稳健的双层电子结构和减少的磁交换#
    Robust Bilayer Electronic Structure and Reduced Magnetic Exchange in La2SmNi2O7#
    作者：Xinru Huang、Jiaxin Yan、Ruixian Liu、Zhijia Zhang、Anni Chen、Yating Luo 等 15 人。Preprints，2026-10-17
    🧲 物理/材料 · 磁性·自旋电子学AI×科学交叉2方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：稀土替代提供了增强双层镍酸盐超导性的有效途径，但其对局部电子结构和磁交换相互作用的影响仍不清楚。在这里，我们报告了 La2SmNi2O7 单晶的 Ni-L₃ 边缘共振非弹性 X 射线散射 (RIXS) 测量，La2SmNi2O7 是一种 Sm 取代的双层镍酸盐，具有增强的面内晶格畸变和减小的层间 Ni--Oᵣₘ AP--Ni 键角。尽管进行了这些结构修改，与 NiO₆ 晶体场和电荷转移过程相关的局域电子激发的能量基本上与 La₃Ni2O₇ 中相同，包括接近 0.3~eV、1.0~eV 和 1.5~eV 的特征。这表明 Sm 取代并没有显着改变局部 NiO6 晶体场和角共享双层的电荷转移能级。相比之下，高分辨率 RIXS 揭示了定义明确的类自旋波激发，其带顶降低了 sim7~meV。自旋激发色散的双层 J_c-J1-J2 海森堡分析表明有效磁交换规模减少了约 10%，这与层间 Ni--Oᵣₘ AP-Ni 键角的减小相关。这些模式的小固有阻尼进一步表明环境压力母相中具有强大的类局部矩自旋动力学。这些结果揭示了对 Sm 替代的响应的二分性，局部电子激发能大部分保留，但磁交换规模减小。他们将 Ni--Oᵣₘ AP--Ni 几何形状确定为影响 Ruddlesden-Popper 镍酸盐磁性的重要结构参数，并为该族高温超导微观模型提供了光谱约束。

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：electronic structure、magnetism。

    📖 英文原文

    Rare-earth substitution provides an effective route to enhance superconductivity in bilayer nickelates, but its influence on the local electronic structure and magnetic exchange interactions remains unclear. Here we report Ni-L₃-edge resonant inelastic x-ray scattering (RIXS) measurements on single crystals of La₂SmNi₂O₇, a Sm-substituted bilayer nickelate with enhanced in-plane lattice distortion and a reduced interlayer Ni--Oᵣₘ AP--Ni bond angle. Despite these structural modifications, the local electronic excitations associated with NiO₆ crystal-field and charge-transfer processes occur at essentially the same energies as in La₃Ni₂O₇, including features near 0.3~eV, 1.0~eV, and 1.5~eV. This shows that Sm substitution does not substantially alter the local NiO₆ crystal-field and charge-transfer energy scales of the corner-shared bilayer. In contrast, high-resolution RIXS reveals well-defined spin-wave-like excitations whose band top is reduced by sim7~meV. A bilayer J_c-J₁-J₂ Heisenberg analysis of the spin excitation dispersion indicates an approximately 10% reduction of the effective magnetic exchange scale, which correlates with the reduced interlayer Ni--Oᵣₘ AP--Ni bond angle. The small intrinsic damping of these modes further points to robust local-moment-like spin dynamics in the ambient-pressure parent phase.These results reveal a dichotomy in the response to Sm substitution, with largely preserved local electronic excitation energies but a reduced magnetic exchange scale. They identify the Ni--Oᵣₘ AP--Ni geometry as an important structural parameter influencing magnetism in Ruddlesden--Popper nickelates and provide spectroscopic constraints on microscopic models of high-T_c superconductivity in this family.

    反应时间和工艺环境条件对Pd-Zr-炭催化剂HTPB多相加氢影响的研究#
    Study on Influence of Reaction time and process environment conditions on Heterogeneous Hydrogenation of HTPB using a Pd-Zr-charcoal catalyst#
    作者：Devi Vara Prasad、S Karunakaran、Kuruvilla Joseph。Preprints，2026-10-17
    🧲 物理/材料 · 磁性·自旋电子学AI×科学交叉2方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：本文概述了反应器环境条件对端羟基聚丁二烯（HTPB）聚合物采用异丙醇和甲苯作为活性溶剂介质的双溶剂体系催化加氢法合成氢化HTPB（HHTPB）的影响。展示了一种新颖的合成方法，以了解各种反应器环境条件（例如氮气、氢气和氢气-空气环境）对使用由钯和锆作为贱金属组成的负载在活性炭上的催化剂氢化 HTPB 的影响。实验在 100 mL Inconel 制造的带有磁力驱动变速叶轮的间歇式反应器中进行。研究中展示了工艺时间的优化并固定为 6 小时。通过 NMR、FTIR 和化学分析对 HTPB 和 HHTPB 聚合物进行了表征。结果表明，在给定的反应器体积和选定的操作条件下，HTPB的加氢度约为87.5%，在氢气-空气环境条件下为蜡状固体。 HTPB加氢后，羟值没有明显变化，在H2环境下形成的HHTPB为乙烯基型功能异构体。在所有情况下，氢化后均观察到分子量略有增加。对 HTPB 和 HHTPB 进行了 TG-DSC 研究，发现 HHTPB 的热稳定性略有增加。

    📖 英文原文

    This paper outlines the influence of reactor environment conditions in synthesizing hydrogenated HTPB (HHTPB) using Hydroxyl-terminated polybutadiene (HTPB) polymer by catalytic hydrogenation method using bi-solvent system using iso-propyl alcohol and toluene as active solvent media. A novel synthesis approach to understand the effect of various reactor environment conditions such as nitrogen gas, H2 gas and hydrogen-air environments to hydrogenate HTPB using a catalyst made up of palladium and zirconium as base metals supported on activated charcoal was demonstrated. Experiments were conducted in a 100 mL Inconel made batch type reactor with a magnetically driven variable speed impeller. Optimization of process time is demonstrated and fixed as 6 h for the study. HTPB and HHTPB polymers were characterized by NMR, FTIR, and chemical analysis. The results showed that the degree of hydrogenation of HTPB is ~ 87.5% which is a waxy solid under hydrogen-air environment conditions for the given reactor volume with the chosen operating conditions. After hydrogenation of HTPB, no significant change in hydroxyl value was noticed, and the HHTPB formed is of vinyl-type functional isomer in H2 environment. A marginal increase in molecular weight was observed after hydrogenation in all cases. HTPB and HHTPB were subjected to TG- DSC studies and it was found that the thermal stability of HHTPB marginally increased.

    📖 英文原文

    Pollution in protected areas can affect ecosystem integrity, biodiversity conservation, and the long-term sustainability of natural resource management. This study developed an integrated GIS and machine learning framework to assess and map pollution vulnerability in South Africa’s Kruger National Park (KNP). Environmental and atmospheric variables, including the Normalised Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), sulphur dioxide (SO₂), carbon monoxide (CO), methane (CH₄), particulate matter (PM₂.₅), rainfall, and temperature, were integrated within Google Earth Engine and Python-based analytical environments. Random Forest (RF), Support Vector Machine (SVM), and Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM) algorithms were applied to classify spatial patterns of pollution vulnerability and identify the variables contributing most strongly to the observed patterns. The results showed that GBM achieved an overall classification accuracy of 0.76, while RF produced an area under the curve (AUC) of 0.96 and SVM achieved an AUC of 0.90. SO₂ and NO₂ were among the most influential variables in the modelling framework, indicating the importance of atmospheric pollution indicators in explaining spatial variations in vulnerability. The resulting vulnerability maps identified areas with moderate to high pollution vulnerability that can support targeted environmental monitoring and conservation planning. By integrating remote sensing, GIS, and machine learning, the study provides a spatial decision-support framework for sustainable environmental management in a large, protected area. The findings contribute particularly to Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 15 (Life on Land) through improved protection and management of terrestrial ecosystems, while also supporting SDG 13 (Climate Action) through climate-sensitive environmental planning and contributing to SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) through improved management of environmental pollution.
