    机器学习潜力的对比正则化#
    Contrastive Regularization of Machine Learning Potentials#
    作者：Dimitrios Tzivrailis、Georgios Sotiropoulos、Alberto Rosso、Eiji Kawasaki。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-10-26
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · AI×物理AI×科学交叉6方向匹配8

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：机器学习模型越来越多地被训练为回归器，然后部署为生成器，从语言模型到学习模拟器。在这种情况下，保留数据的准确性几乎不能说明模型在采样时产生的分布。我们研究这个问题，其中正确的分布是由第一原理确定的，在机器学习原子间势（MLIP）中，通过最小化密度泛函理论（DFT）能量和力的均方误差（MSE）来训练，然后用于运行分子模拟。 MSE 永远不会限制势所定义的玻尔兹曼分布，因此 MLIP 可以匹配保留的 DFT 数据，并且在采样时仍然会漂移到破坏其热力学的虚假最小值。基于统计物理学的变分原理，我们引入了对比正则化 MSE (CRMSE)，这是一个训练后步骤，其中势充当其自己的基于能量的模型，并学习重现其生成的分布，而无需任何新的 DFT 数据。对于 MD17 基准的分子，CRMSE 恢复物理采样并恢复 DFT 自由能分布，同时将能量和力误差保持在化学精度范围内。我们的结果表明，要采样的模型必须对其产生的分布进行训练，而不仅仅是为了逐点精度。

    🔗 与我们工作的关联与启发

    关联：与你方向的关键词重合：machine learning potential、density functional theory、interatomic potential。

    📖 英文原文

    Machine learning models are increasingly trained as regressors and then deployed as generators, from language models to learned simulators. In this setting, accuracy on held-out data says little about the distribution a model produces when it is sampled. We study this problem where the correct distribution is fixed by first principles, in machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) trained by minimizing the mean squared error (MSE) on density functional theory (DFT) energies and forces, then used to run molecular simulations. The MSE never constrains the Boltzmann distribution that the potential defines, so an MLIP can match held-out DFT data and still drift, when sampled, into spurious minima that corrupt its thermodynamics. Building on the variational principle of statistical physics, we introduce Contrastive Regularized MSE (CRMSE), a post-training step in which the potential acts as its own energy-based model and learns to reproduce the distribution it generates, without any new DFT data. On molecules from the MD17 benchmark, CRMSE restores physical sampling and recovers DFT free-energy profiles, while keeping energy and force errors within chemical accuracy. Our results suggest that a model meant to be sampled must be trained on the distribution it produces, not only for pointwise accuracy.

    📖 英文原文

    Mapping the phase diagram of a new quantum Hamiltonian demands expert judgement at every step: which observables to measure, where in parameter space to compute, or how to separate genuine order from finite-size artefacts. We introduce QuantumMatterExplorer, an autonomous agent that takes only a Hamiltonian and its parameter ranges as input and returns a phase diagram obtained from its own numerical simulations. The agent is built on a large language model and is designed to reason over a long horizon, maintain a persistent written plan, and drive a set of computational tools, with density-matrix renormalization group (DMRG) calculations cross-checked against independent exact diagonalization. The agent combines a large language model with persistent planning, a library of expert computational-physics skills, and density-matrix renormalization group calculations validated against independent exact diagonalization. It adaptively selects quantities to evaluate, refines parameter scans, and performs finite-size analyses as evidence accumulates. After validating the approach on Hamiltonians with known transitions, we challenge it with a frustrated bilinear-biquadratic spin-1 chain with single-ion anisotropy, whose two-dimensional phase diagram was published only recently and is unlikely to appear in any training corpus. Given no hint about the expected physics, the agent proposes which observables to probe, and identifies candidate Néel, Haldane and dimerized phases, in broad agreement with the reference phase diagram. It also detects incommensurate correlations and infers the frustration-dependent geometry of the phase boundaries, all within an eight-hour compute budget. These results open a practical route to the autonomous computational discovery of new phases of quantum matter.

    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉6方向匹配3

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：硫化物固体电解质由于其高锂离子电导率和良好的加工性能而对全固态电池有吸引力，但其实际应用受到电化学不稳定性和湿气引起的降解的限制。分子表面涂层提供了一种可扩展的策略来提高耐湿性和界面稳定性，但对控制表面相互作用的分子水平因素的了解有限。通过将机器学习潜力实现的大规模原子模拟与目标实验相结合，我们提出了一个用于设计 Li₆PS₅Cl (LPSCl) 分子涂层的综合框架。使用在涵盖 LPSCl 表面、分子吸附物及其相互作用的 r²SCAN 参考数据集上微调的 PET-MAD 原子间势，我们模拟了具有不同长度烷基链的羧酸 (CA)、磺酸 (SA) 和膦酸 (PA) 的表面吸附以及这些涂层表面与水在有限温度下的相互作用。模拟表明，锂化头基通常表现出更有利的吸附能。阻抗谱表明，粉末加工后，锂化涂层仍保持初始离子电导率的40--55%。虽然吸附强度主要由头基化学和锂化状态控制，但涂层形态主要取决于烷基链长度和表面覆盖率。特别是，长链锂化羧酸盐形成致密的刷状疏水层，减少水进入活性锂表面位点，并在涂层厚度约为 0.8 nm 时提供保护。与这些预测一致，较长的羧酸盐链可以更有效地抑制 H2S 的释放。由于较厚的涂层也会阻碍锂离子传输，因此中等链长可在防潮和离子电导率之间提供最佳平衡。

    📖 英文原文

    Sulfide solid electrolytes are attractive for all-solid-state batteries because of their high Li-ion conductivity and favorable processability, but their practical use is limited by electrochemical instability and moisture-induced degradation. Molecular surface coatings offer a scalable strategy to improve humidity tolerance and interfacial stability, yet there is limited understanding of the molecular-level factors governing surface interactions. By combining large-scale atomistic simulations enabled by a machine-learning potential with targeted experiments, we present a comprehensive framework for designing molecular coatings on Li₆PS₅Cl (LPSCl). Using a PET-MAD interatomic potential fine-tuned on an r²SCAN reference dataset covering LPSCl surfaces, molecular adsorbates, and their interactions, we model surface adsorption of carboxylic (CA), sulfonic (SA), and phosphonic (PA) acids with alkyl chains of varying lengths and the interaction of these coated surfaces with water at finite temperature. The simulations show that lithiated head groups generally exhibit more favorable adsorption energies. Impedance spectroscopy shows that the lithiated coatings maintain 40--55% of the initial ionic conductivity after powder processing. While adsorption strength is largely controlled by head-group chemistry and lithiation state, coating morphology depends primarily on alkyl-chain length and surface coverage. In particular, long-chain lithiated carboxylates form dense, brush-like hydrophobic layers that reduce water access to reactive Li surface sites, with protection setting in at a coating thickness of approximately 0.8 nm. Consistent with these predictions, longer carboxylate chains suppress H₂S evolution more effectively. Because thicker coatings also hinder Li-ion transport, intermediate chain lengths offer the best balance between moisture protection and ionic conductivity.

    探索晶体材料正向筛选和逆向设计的极限#
    Probing the limits of forward screening and inverse design of crystal materials#
    作者：Zhilong Song、Zijun Pang、Lixue Cheng。arXiv · cond-mat，2026-10-26
    🔬 神经网络势·电子结构 · 其他AI×科学交叉6方向匹配0

    📄 摘要（中文翻译）：晶体材料的设计要么是通过筛选已知晶体的目标特性，要么是通过生成具有该特性的新晶体来设计。两条路线都是根据已知晶体的公共数据库进行判断的，但这些数据库对同一晶体的命名不同。最近的生成和自主发现活动宣布的几种新材料已被证明是已知的阶段或它们的密切变体。两条路线的极限仍未测量。在这里，我们通过将 55 个数据库中的晶体按结构匹配到一个包含 1460 万个晶体和 1.318 亿个属性值的自扩展数据库中来探索它们，每个数据库都可以追溯到其来源。只有 4.5% 的晶体属性对具有价值。为了填补空对，我们开发了 CGTNet，这是一种等变图转换器，在两个公共基准测试的多项任务上具有最先进的精度。经过每项任务的训练，它形成了一个属性模型库，我们将发布其预测。前瞻性筛选的范围被低估了。已知的晶体含有数以万计的超不可压缩候选晶体，但没有记录体积模量，对合成证据的事后审核发现其中有 ReN2，其体积模量测得为 428 GPa。逆向设计所声称的新颖性被高估了。在相同的验证预算下，刚度调节发生器返回比筛选更坚硬的撞击（100 比 55），但全部都含有锇和 82 个重复记录的成分。因此，整合和完善已知晶体的数据库突破了这两种途径的限制：它扩大了筛选范围，并设定了判断所生成晶体是否为新晶体的参考。

    📖 英文原文

    Crystal materials are designed either by screening known crystals for a target property or by generating new crystals with that property. Both routes are judged against public databases of known crystals, but these databases name the same crystal differently. Several materials announced as new by recent generative and autonomous discovery campaigns have since proved to be known phases or close variants of them. The limits of both routes remain unmeasured. Here we probe them by matching the crystals of 55 databases by structure into one self-extending database of 14.6 million crystals and 131.8 million property values, each traced to its source. Only 4.5% of its crystal-property pairs carry a value.
